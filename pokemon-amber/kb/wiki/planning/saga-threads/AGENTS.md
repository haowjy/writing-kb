# saga-threads/AGENTS.md

This folder is reserved for the few story-level throughlines that shape the
whole saga. A saga thread carries a central unresolved conflict, promise,
relationship, or mystery across major arcs and changes both plot outcomes and
the protagonist's development.

## Admission Test

A page belongs here only when all are true:

1. It remains unresolved across major arc boundaries.
2. It has setup, escalation, and eventual payoff.
3. It materially changes Amber or the larger political/world situation.
4. Removing it would force several major arcs to be restructured.
5. It cannot be contained adequately in one arc or character page.

Recurring details, individual scenes, local mysteries, and small promises do
not qualify. Put them in the relevant numbered arc folder. Character-only
psychology belongs in wiki/reference/characters/.
