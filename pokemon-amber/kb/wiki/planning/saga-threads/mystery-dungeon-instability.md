# Mystery Dungeon Instability and Exploration

**Status:** drafting

Mystery Dungeons are ancient natural phenomena. Human activity is worsening
their instability, while more severe incidents and newly recognized sites
create the appearance of spread. The exact cosmology behind that apparent
spread remains open. The thread begins with local expeditions and containment
and ends with Amber pursuing Giovanni through Ultra Space.

## Crisis Model

- Modern activity destabilizes existing Dungeons; it does not create them.
- The working outbreak direction allows distorted terrain, local weather or
  habitat effects, displaced or aggressive Pokemon, and looping geography to
  escape a Dungeon's known boundary into routes, settlements, and habitats.
- Expeditions can "defeat" a Dungeon colloquially, but operational success
  means containing an outbreak, rescuing victims, removing aggravating factors,
  and forcing the effects back. Permanent destruction remains unknown.
- The crisis functions like global warming: damage accumulates across many
  sources while researchers, governments, and industries dispute responsibility.
- Possible pressures include ecological destruction, intensive energy
  extraction, high-energy experiments, Ultra Wormhole research, disruption of
  legendary Pokemon, and wartime production. Their relative importance remains
  open.
- No single Pokemon, mastermind, or prophecy causes the crisis. Old flood and
  end-of-world traditions may describe it without literally predicting one
  fixed calamity.

Mystery Dungeons are not Ultra Wormholes. Dungeons distort places within the
Pokemon world; Wormholes connect worlds. Argaea has its own sealed-realm
mechanism. Laqua is neither their source nor a universal dimensional answer.

## Evidence and Disclosure

The pattern emerges through accumulated field reports, ecological research,
and recurring disasters. Public researchers can establish the broad danger
before anyone can prove Rocket's contribution.

Rocket eventually develops stronger internal evidence that its work is
worsening the instability. Giovanni continues anyway. Readers should infer
this from Rocket's choices and the timing of events rather than being told.
Late scientific files recovered from a Rocket base or archive may confirm what
Giovanni knew. The investigator, location, records, and timing remain open.

Rocket accelerates the crisis; it did not create Mystery Dungeons and is not
necessarily the only modern contributor.

## Institutional Response

About ten years before the story, worsening incidents led reputable local
guilds, the Joy/Pokemon Center network, Rangers, and governments to establish a
newer international [adventurer federation](../../reference/organizations/adventurer-federation.md).
It pools requests, maps, field reports, expeditions, rescue support, warnings,
equipment, and cross-border logistics so outbreaks can be contained before
they spread into inhabited terrain.

The response remains layered: adventurers conduct exploration and contracted
fieldwork; Rangers manage ecosystems, closures, wild Pokemon, and public
safety; the [International Police](../../reference/organizations/international-police.md)
investigates crimes or suspects spanning jurisdictions.

## Exploration Throughline

- Alonso and adventurer culture introduce Amber to explicit Dungeon
  exploration through existing quests and Ranger procedures.
- Amber gains field knowledge, navigation skill, and research experience.
  Whether she eventually claims the adventurer identity remains open.
- Argaea provides experience with dangerous sealed space without becoming a
  Dungeon or alternate universe.
- Alola and Aether introduce true cross-world travel. Rocket later appropriates
  their Ultra Wormhole research.
- After the main-world war, Amber and a small group pursue Giovanni through
  Ultra Space. The final saga turns the exploration thread outward into many
  different Pokemon worlds.

These threads converge without sharing one cosmological cause.

## Open Decisions

- Exact causes, thresholds, and reversibility.
- Literal physical expansion or new land versus destabilization of known sites
  plus discovery of previously unknown Dungeons.
- Which dungeon-break effects become fixed rules rather than working influence.
- Whether any Dungeon can be permanently destroyed.
- Whether the crisis produces one discrete global disaster.
- Its timing relative to the main-world war.
- The truth behind old flood and end-of-world traditions.
- Laqua's eventual role.
- Amber's relationship to the adventurer identity.
