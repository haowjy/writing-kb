# Mystery Dungeon Instability and Exploration

**Status:** drafting

Human activity worsens ancient Mystery Dungeons until scattered hazards become
a global ecological crisis. The thread begins with expeditions and containment
and ends with Amber pursuing Giovanni through Ultra Space. [Mystery
Dungeons](../../reference/world/phenomena/mystery-dungeons.md) owns the working
mechanics.

## Evidence and Disclosure

Field reports, research, and recurring disasters establish the pattern before
anyone can prove Rocket's contribution. Rocket later develops stronger internal
evidence and Giovanni continues anyway. Readers should infer this before
recovered scientific files confirm what he knew; the investigator, archive, and
timing remain open.

Rocket accelerates the crisis but did not create Dungeons and is not necessarily
the only modern contributor. Old flood or end-of-world traditions may describe
the crisis without predicting one fixed calamity.

## Exploration Throughline

- Alonso and adventurer culture introduce Amber to explicit Dungeon exploration.
- Amber gains field, navigation, and research experience; whether she claims
  the adventurer identity remains open.
- Ashura contributes sealed-space experience without becoming a Dungeon.
- Alola introduces cross-world travel and Rocket's later wormhole capability.
- After the main-world war, Amber and a small group pursue Giovanni through
  Ultra Space into the Rainbow Rocket endgame.

These threads converge without sharing one cosmological cause.

## Open Decisions

- exact destabilization thresholds, effects, reversibility, and whether any
  Dungeon can be permanently destroyed;
- literal expansion versus destabilization and discovery;
- whether one discrete global disaster occurs and its timing relative to war;
- the truth behind old traditions and Laqua's eventual role;
- Amber's relationship to adventurer identity.
