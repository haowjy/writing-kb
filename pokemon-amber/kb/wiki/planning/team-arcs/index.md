# Pokemon Team Arcs

Relationship and character arcs for Pokemon attached to recurring trainers. These pages answer how a Pokemon changes, how its trainer misreads or helps it, and where that relationship resolves. Character facts belong in Pokemon reference; membership logistics belong in rosters; chronological placement belongs in regional arc plans.
