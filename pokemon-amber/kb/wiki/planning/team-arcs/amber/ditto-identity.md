# Ditto: Identity and Agency

## Arc in One Line

A Pokemon trained to become whatever Fuji commanded chooses what it will become, what it will refuse, and Amber as its partner.

Pallet already completed that. The journey does not automatically get a second identity plot.

## Established

- Fuji trained it as a transformation weapon. Capabilities: [Ditto](../../../reference/characters/pokemon/amber-team/ditto.md).
- It weakened the attack on Stephen, refused the finishing command, and later refused lethal orders involving Amber and Oak. [ch13] [ch15] [ch16]
- The bond with Amber formed without ceremony. It chose her over Fuji. Oak gave it to her before the journey. [ch17]
- Playfulness is Mew-nature. Amber gave it room; she did not teach it.
- Amber was not present for Stephen's death. There is no timeskip plot of her blaming Ditto for it. If she ever gets those facts, that is a later scene.

## Journey state

- Mellowed. Can be careless. Still a protector, not always acting like one.
- Buffer: what it wants/can hold; fades in 1–3 days; ~10 before jumble. Fuji's list is gone. Ditto does not want that drilling.
- Ranch sharing already happened. Jealousy of Amber paying attention to other Pokemon is not a new beat. If jealousy exists, it has to be about a Pokemon joining *her party*.
- Ditto is not Amber's universal tactical answer. Agency is refusal, form choice, and whether to recopy something dangerous.
- No delayed Arcanine-form reveal. Dropped.
- The Mew connection, if named at all, is much later — not Kanto year 1.
- Amber treats Ditto as a normal game-Ditto: transform what's in front of you. She does not know it can hold forms. She finds that out on the road after the timeskip, not during the ranch years. She still does not know why. Ditto does not want protocol drilling.

## Not decided

- Continuous growth vs stable partner.
- Later turn: after Amber is in real danger, Ditto chooses to get dangerous again. "Weapon" means combat-serious unless the author reopens the ch13 kill-floor.
- Jealousy of new teammates. Ch19's throw can stay food-guarding.
- Whether training (or emotion) can raise the 10-form / 1–3 day cap, including a bind Fuji never unlocked. Do not put that power on the page until chosen.
- Exact jumble behavior at 10.
- Which road scene is the first time she notices a form persist without the model. Same-fight copies (Pidgey then Caterpie in one battle) do not prove it.
