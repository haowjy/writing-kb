# Charcadet / Ceruledge: Choice, Theft, and Attempted Recovery

## Arc in One Line

Charcadet chooses Amber after watching how she acts, is later stolen and shaped into Ceruledge by Team Rocket, and becomes the personal loss that radicalizes Amber.

## Relationship Movement

1. Alonso brings the younger Charcadet to Kanto.
2. Charcadet observes Amber protect Teddiursa and continue honoring the missing-Ursaring promise.
3. Its choice develops across most of the Kanto journey rather than an instant camp capture; Charcadet remains Alonso's Pokemon during that time.
4. Near the end of Kanto, Alonso recognizes and permits Charcadet's eventual choice of Amber.
5. Charcadet becomes Amber's Pokemon only shortly before 4Ever.
6. Rocket steals Charcadet before Amber's first conference and evolves it into Ceruledge under coercive conditions.
7. Amber enters the conference without her ace and becomes increasingly willing to pursue Rocket personally.
8. Ceruledge recurs as a Rocket asset Amber recognizes. Recovery, memory, and reconciliation remain unresolved.

## Boundary With Saga Planning

This page owns Charcadet's relationship with Amber. The cross-arc Rocket consequences, armor meaning, theft campaign, and eventual recovery problem remain in [the Charcadet/Ceruledge saga thread](../../saga-threads/charcadet-ceruledge.md).
