# Heracross — Brawler Identity and Ranged Precision

Heracross wants to fight, but it is physically weak by Heracross standards. Its
arc is not learning courage: it must stop treating its species' expected
charging, wrestling, and melee identity as the only respectable way to fight.

## Arrival

Amber captures Heracross by a conventional conscious battle capture in the
voluntary, level-appropriate Grass Mystery Dungeon after Ch. 26--27. Heracross
charges Amber's group with complete confidence, is easily overpowered despite
the species' extraordinary-strength reputation, gets up, and charges again.
The deadpan mismatch is the introduction; exact battle and capture choreography
remain open.

Amber had released a newly caught Pidgey expecting to make room for a
Grass-type Rock counter before Pewter. Heracross is the actual answer, not a
separate Grass capture: it is Bug/Fighting, takes neutral Rock damage, and its
Fighting offense is super-effective against Rock.

## Character Movement

- Heracross starts fights and repeatedly chooses conventional charging,
  wrestling, and close-range attacks.
- It keeps losing because it measures itself against what a Heracross is
  supposed to be, not because it is afraid to act. This is deliberately unlike
  Caterpie's cowardice-and-courage arc.
- It has a real talent for precise, long-range physical repeated-hit attacks.
  Amber helps it recognize that style as its own strength rather than a
  consolation prize.

Candidate tools include *Bullet Seed*, *Pin Missile*, *Rock Blast*, and thrown
or otherwise used environmental projectiles. Their availability, timing, and
exact move acquisition remain open.

## Later Payoffs

A later anti-poaching or forest-defense spotlight lets Heracross voluntarily
defend a grove or nest with its chosen ranged style. It is neither its
acquisition nor a reason for it to leave Amber.

Heracross later gains access to Heracrossite. Mega Heracross's raw strength
creates a temptation to discard the specialization it learned in favor of the
brawler power it once envied. The mature payoff combines Mega strength and
Skill Link with its established precision.
