# Vee / Espeon: Shared Enemy, Trust, and Stability

## Arc in One Line

A Rocket experiment forced through painful identities first allies with Amber against a shared enemy, then slowly chooses trust and a stable Espeon form.

## Starting State

- Vee is an Eevee modified by Team Rocket to cycle painfully among Vaporeon, Jolteon, and Flareon.
- It mistrusts humans and is first seeded through the Route 7 rogue-Eeveelution notice.
- Its changing body resonates with Amber's identity themes without reducing either character to a direct metaphor for the other.

## Relationship Movement

1. Amber encounters Vee in Celadon before 4Ever.
2. Vee initially bonds more readily with Amber's Pokemon, likely Ditto, than with Amber.
3. Amber helps repel a Rocket attempt to recapture Vee, then leaves without recruiting it.
4. After Charcadet's theft, Amber deliberately returns to Celadon seeking Vee.
5. Vee joins because both now have the same enemy; warmth and trust come later.
6. Its eventual permanent evolution into Espeon sacrifices painful multi-form flexibility for stability.

## Open Questions

- Exact first-encounter mechanics and which Pokemon Vee trusts first.
- What makes Vee accept Amber's deliberate return.
- Timing and cause of permanent Espeon evolution.
- Whether stability is medically engineered, emotional, evolutionary, or some combination.
