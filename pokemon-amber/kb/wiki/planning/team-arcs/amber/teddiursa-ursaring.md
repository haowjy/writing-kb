# Teddiursa / Ursaring: Promise, Grief, and Vengeance

## Arc in One Line

Amber's promise to help Teddiursa save his missing mother binds them together; her eventual death transforms him into an Ursaring consumed by the same vengeance hardening Amber.

## Relationship Movement

1. Teddiursa attacks humans because Team Rocket is tied to his mother's disappearance.
2. Amber identifies the actual problem and promises to help find Ursaring.
3. Teddiursa tolerates Amber because she moves toward the problem, not because he instantly trusts her.
4. Amber protects him at the Rocket camp before she has time to rationalize the choice.
5. He nearly reunites with his mother in the cave, but Rocket removes her again.
6. The unfulfilled promise becomes a recurring personal debt between Teddy and Amber.
7. During 4Ever, Teddiursa finds his mother corrupted beyond saving; she dies and he evolves through grief.
8. Ursaring's pursuit of strength and vengeance mirrors Amber's radicalization after Charcadet's theft.
9. Both eventually need to relinquish destructive versions of their grief.
