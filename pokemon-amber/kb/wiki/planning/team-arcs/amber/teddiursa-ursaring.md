# Teddiursa / Ursaring: Promise, Grief, and Vengeance

## Arc in One Line

Amber's commitment to help Teddiursa becomes a promise to save his mother once the cave reveals captive Ursaring; her eventual death transforms him into an Ursaring consumed by the same vengeance hardening Amber.

## Relationship Movement

1. Teddiursa flees Team Rocket onto maintained Route 1; Rocket captured his mother when she resisted their seizure of their home cave.
2. Amber intervenes before understanding the situation; Teddiursa turns back to fight beside her and Ditto.
3. Teddiursa communicates his ask through gesture and pantomime --- "help me" as a concept, not speech --- and Amber patiently decodes it and promises to help save Ursaring.
4. Teddiursa tolerates Amber because she moves toward the problem, not because he instantly trusts her.
5. He reaches his mother captive and rampaging in the cave, but Rocket extracts her through a prepared escape route and collapses the way out.
6. The unfulfilled promise becomes a recurring personal debt between Teddy and Amber.
7. During 4Ever, Teddiursa finds his mother corrupted beyond saving; she dies and he evolves through grief.
8. Ursaring's pursuit of strength and vengeance mirrors Amber's radicalization after Charcadet's theft.
9. Both eventually need to relinquish destructive versions of their grief.
