# Project X Planning Questions

**Status:** speculation

The [Project X reference](../reference/organizations/project-x.md) owns the
settled compact, including Rowan's membership, Oak's formal exclusion, Rowan as
the source of Oak's initial knowledge, and their compartmentalized covert
consultation. The operational details that could change its story role remain
open:

- founding date and whether Lance founded it or joined it;
- whether Lance represents Indigo, Blackthorn, or both, and his authority;
- exact administration and the rest of the membership roster;
- the precise timing and volume of Rowan's later disclosures to Oak; and
- whether Rowan is ever exposed and, if so, how the resulting political rupture
  unfolds.
