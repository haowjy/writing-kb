# Project X Planning Questions

**Status:** speculation

[Project X](../reference/organizations/project-x.md) is settled as a secret
elite military-strengthening compact. This page owns the unresolved choices
about its origin, administration, and roster so that possibilities are not
mistaken for established organization reference.

## Open Decisions

- Exact founding date and whether Project X predates Lance's Championship.
- Whether Lance personally started it or joined an existing compact.
- Whether Lance represents Indigo, Blackthorn, or both in practice.
- Whether Blackthorn leads the compact.
- Exact administration and membership list.
- The timing and terms of Lance's invitation to Oak.

## Discussed, Not Selected

The following are possible participants or traditions, not a roster:

- Blackthorn Dragon Clan;
- Ecruteak houses;
- Kalos government or Tower traditions;
- a possible Pryce/Mahogany ice clan;
- Diamond/Pearl successor traditions;
- Celestic custodians;
- the Stone family;
- Opelucid lineage.

Pryce's possible clan role or exclusion is not settled. The discussed
Oak--Pryce--Lance succession and Lance's father as an intermediary are also not
selected; they do not replace the [current Kanto-Johto history](../reference/world/history/kanto-johto-unification-war.md).

## Constraint

Do not turn this list into a complete international council. The organization
reference commits only to a selective class of participating powers and to the
compact's elite military purpose.
