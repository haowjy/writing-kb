# Trainer-Start Age and Peer Cohort

**Status:** drafting

Amber, Kyle, Sabrina, Reid, and Nora graduate middle school normally at age 14 and begin their relevant trainer phase at 14. Amber, Reid, and Nora are the same graduating cohort.

Amber is one year older than Ash and Gary. At Amber's graduation and departure, Ash and Gary are 13 and have one school year left.

Oak rigorously prepares and sponsors Amber, but that support is not an early-graduation or early-departure exception. Kyle's Dojo training and Sabrina's prodigy status likewise do not create early-departure exceptions. This records these characters' settled story path; it does not make a universal claim about every trainer.

Kanto Year 1 begins when Amber is 14. Later age labels follow from that start while preserving the existing event order.
