# Ashura Visit 1 Succession Model

**Status:** drafting

No Visit 1 succession model is selected. Current planning must not state the old Mad-King death/escape/seal sequence as fact.

## Conflict to Resolve

| Legacy model present in older plans | Newer direction under consideration |
|---|---|
| Alexander enters Ashura with Amber, kills his mad or corrupted father, Mega Darkrai exits the body, and Cresselia plus Gardevoir create a temporary seal. | An uncle usurps the throne before or around the outside contact; Alexander/the heir vanishes; Cresselia does not acknowledge the usurper and disappears. |

The models have incompatible premises. Do not blend their events into a single current narrative.

## Directional Constraints

- Ashura's regime may study or attempt to harness Mega Darkrai and discover the outside world through a barrier breach.
- Infiltration and reconnaissance are more credible early external actions than immediate conquest.
- Unrest may consolidate under a regime rather than persist as a Seven/Three Kingdoms-style fragmentation; neither political end state is chosen.
- Mega Darkrai **may** amplify existing desires rather than simply make people evil. This is a proposed rule, not settled canon.
- The Loyal Three are corrupted. Their corruption source is open; Pecharunt and Toxic Chains are canonical Pokemon lore, not this story's established mechanism.

## Required Before Locking Visit 1

Choose the succession model, Alexander's escape/return chronology, Cresselia's role, Mega Darkrai's state, and the exact first-contact stakes. Then replace the contradiction rather than retaining both as active directions.
