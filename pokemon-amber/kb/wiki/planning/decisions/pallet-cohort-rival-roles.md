# Pallet Cohort Rival Roles

**Status:** drafting

Oak sponsors exactly three Pallet graduates in this cohort: Amber, Reid, and
Nora. All three are fourteen.

Chapter 18 has an author-confirmed direction but remains an unapproved draft;
these are planning decisions, not published events.

Reid is the only newly introduced conventional recurring/tournament rival
currently promised for Amber. Nora is an analytical academic foil who may
recur, but she is not promised as a major battle rival or tournament fixture.

## Distinct Roles

| Character | Settled role | Not implied |
|---|---|---|
| Reid | Fiery Oak-sponsored Pallet contemporary and conventional recurring/tournament rival; receives Cyndaquil | Later team, type focus, career, or exact tournament cadence/outcome |
| Nora | Analytical academic foil and Oak-sponsored member of Amber's graduating cohort; receives Eevee; may recur | Major battle rivalry, recurring tournament opposition, later team, career, or any tournament participation |

"Academic foil" describes how Nora contrasts with Amber's approach to school
and analysis. It is not a synonym for "battle rival." Likewise, membership in
Amber's cohort establishes shared history, not a promise that every classmate
will become a recurring competitor.

## Planned Chapter 18 Treatment

Chapter 18 opens after the cohort's normal school graduation, which remains
off-page, with Oak's welcome/adventure speech closely alluding to the Pokemon
games. Keep "The world which you are about to enter" unglossed. Recognition of
the allusion belongs entirely to the reader, not to Amber explicitly noticing
or remembering it; this constraint is limited to the opening speech/button and
does not restrict other game-memory use later in the chapter.

Each graduate steps to a podium with a large red A button. Pressing it unlocks
or reveals a ceremonial Pokeball; the exact compartment engineering remains an
execution choice. "Press the A Button, and let your adventure begin!" works as
Oak's actual ceremony dialogue. All three partners emerge together: Reid grins
over Cyndaquil, Nora cuddles Eevee, and Ditto dives to Amber's face before
wrapping around her like a scarf. Oak explains each partner afterward, with
Amber able to chime in from ranch familiarity. Do not turn the opening into
three separate hand-to-arm transfers or begin it with Reid's smoking-sleeve
joke.

Amber already partners with Ditto, and the ceremony recognizes that existing
partnership. Temporary placement in the ceremony Pokeball is not a custody
transfer, reunion, or first permission for Amber to keep Ditto.

Their trainer credentials and the cohort-specific three prototype Pokedex
handoff follow the partner explanations as already planned.

Reid's competitive charge should promise future encounters, but no Indigo
Plateau finals vow or tournament outcome is required. Nora's precise,
inquisitive exchange supports her analytical role without making her a battle
rival. Kyle is not part of this handoff: his separate Oak visit is specifically
for Riolu, and he meets Amber later on Route 1 in Chapter 21.

The chapter is an adventure comeback whose promise is the road ahead, not a
goodbye or trauma-closure chapter.
