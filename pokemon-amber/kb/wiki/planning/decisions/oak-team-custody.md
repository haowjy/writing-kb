# Oak Team Custody and the Exceptional Starter Offer

**Status:** drafting

Oak's permitted team remains useful for research and ordinary protection without
restoring the politically symbolic force of his secession-war command. The
postwar restriction on Charizard, Venusaur, and Blastoise persists until his
later partial rehabilitation, after which their lines make a one-time starter
offer possible for Ash's cohort.

## Present Team and Custody

Oak's permitted active team is **Dragonite, Arcanine, Gyarados, Tauros, and
Exeggutor**, alongside assorted research Pokemon that are not part of his former
Champion battle force. Gyarados belongs on this roster because of Oak's
established seafaring history.

**Charizard, Venusaur, and Blastoise** fought together under Champion Oak in the
Kanto secession war. Together, they represent the former wartime Champion force
that could be read as a remobilization signal. The terms of Oak's pardon prevent
him from reclaiming or remobilizing the trio at present.

The distinction is intentional: Oak may retain the practical capacity to do
research, travel, and provide ordinary protection, but not the politically
charged combination that would restore his wartime symbolism.

## Partial Rehabilitation and Starter Eggs

Later, Oak materially helps expose Giovanni or cripple Team Rocket. Authorities
then relax the trio's restriction and permit a reunion. The exact operation,
public exposure, and legal mechanism are open; do not imply that any particular
trial, disclosure, or official act has been decided.

After the restriction loosens, League-managed breeding produces **one egg from
each restricted starter line**. Oak receives and raises the Charmander,
Bulbasaur, and Squirtle eggs, then offers those three Pokemon to Ash's cohort.
Do not settle the restricted Pokemon's sexes or their breeding partners.

This is an exceptional consequence of Oak's partial political rehabilitation,
not a yearly Pallet Lab starter giveaway or a standing rare-starter breeding
pipeline. Ash's late arrival, whether Ash takes a starter, and the Pikachu
outcome remain open planning.
