# Arc 16 --- Argaea: Deliberate Return

**Status:** drafting

**Arc:** 16 (Visit 2, Amber age ~18)

This is the deliberate return to the crisis created by Visit 1. Shared history,
barrier mechanics, and the cross-arc promise belong in the [Argaea saga
thread](../../saga-threads/argaea.md).

## Current Direction

Alexander has gained the resolve to re-enter Argaea. He and Amber recruit a
capable group and return deliberately.

The return has three fixed objectives:

1. repair or replace the failing stopgap;
2. free Gardevoir from the external-anchor burden;
3. resolve the greater Argaea / Mega Darkrai crisis.

The exact party, purification or resealing mechanism, fate of the Mad King,
and final win condition remain open. Cresselia plus Shaymin is not a locked
solution, and Alexander reclaiming the throne is not a locked ending.

## Open

- The recruited party.
- The purification, repair, or resealing mechanism.
- The Mad King's fate.
- The final win condition and political outcome.
