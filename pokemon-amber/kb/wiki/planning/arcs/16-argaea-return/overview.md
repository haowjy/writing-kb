# Arc 16 --- Argaea: Deliberate Return

**Status:** drafting

**Arc:** 16 (Visit 2, Amber age ~18)

Alexander and Amber deliberately return with a capable group to:

1. repair or replace the failing seal;
2. free Gardevoir from the external-anchor burden;
3. resolve the Mega Darkrai crisis.

The Mad King is dead, Mega Darkrai remains loose, and the resulting power vacuum
may have become civil war. Roughly seven territorial warlords and Alexander as
unifier are provisional, not settled.

The party, repair or purification mechanism, final win condition, and political
outcome remain open. Cresselia plus Shaymin is not a locked solution, and
Alexander reclaiming the throne is not a locked ending.
