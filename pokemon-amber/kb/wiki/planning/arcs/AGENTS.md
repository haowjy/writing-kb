# arcs/AGENTS.md

Arcs are the book's grain. The list is changeable.

## Levels

| Level | Job | Where |
|---|---|---|
| Book list | Grand structure, subject to change | [index.md](index.md), [saga overview](../saga-overview.md) |
| Book | One arc's purpose, sequence, entry/exit, open questions | `overview.md` in this folder |
| Episode | A named cluster of chapters, not a single chapter | Listed on that arc's overview; chapter files stay in the story repo |

Arc 0 is ch1–17.5 (The Perfect Family). Arc 1 starts at the post-timeskip journey (ch18). The four Pallet years sit between those books; they are not an Arc 0 episode.

Arc 1 is episodic. Plan the named episodes. Do not over-plot the year as one through-line.

What later books need from a finished (or closed) arc lives beside that overview as handoff material, not in chapter notes as a second bible. Chapter `notes.md` pulls from that handoff and from `story/chN/summary.md`.

Minor threads stay inside their arc folder. Do not promote them to saga threads
unless removing them would restructure several major arcs.

Use zero-padded numeric prefixes so folders sort in story order. A range such as
07-09-hoenn/ is allowed when one planning document deliberately spans several
adjacent arcs.
