# Ashura Visit 1: Kitakami

**Status:** drafting — placement and succession model open

A first Ashura visit may include Kitakami and the Ogerpon/Loyal Three crisis, but no current plan fixes its absolute arc number, entry sequence, royal confrontation, or exit mechanism. The directory's old `10-` prefix is legacy organization, not chronology.

## Retained Direction

- Ashura is a physically and dimensionally sealed region in the Pokemon world; Kitakami is a community or province within it.
- Amber and Alexander may enter together by accident. That remains a direction, not a locked access event.
- The Loyal Three are corrupted opponents. Their source is open.
- Mew and Amber's trace Mew DNA do not supply an automatic access or sealing mechanism.

## Deliberately Unresolved

Older plans assert that Alexander kills a Mad King, Mega Darkrai exits the body, and Cresselia/Gardevoir make a temporary seal. A newer usurper-before-entry direction conflicts with that sequence. Neither is current Visit 1 fact; see the [succession model](../../decisions/ashura-visit-one-succession-model.md).

This means the first visit's timing, party, Alexander's knowledge, Cresselia's role, Mega Evolution trigger, escape, and any later rescue obligation are all open.
