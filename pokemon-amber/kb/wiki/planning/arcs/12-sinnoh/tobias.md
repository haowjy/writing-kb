# Tobias — Sinnoh Standard Conference Possibility

**Status:** speculation

Tobias is a possible Ashuran outward-facing competitor in Sinnoh's Standard Conference. He is useful only if he brings pressure from an opening Ashura without prematurely resolving Ashura's politics or turning the Conference into an ACE event.

## Working Possibilities

- He may be Alexander's cousin and the usurper's son.
- He may have crossed into the outside world as a scout or representative after the barrier breach, possibly unaware that Alexander survived.
- He may seek public credentials, fame, independent mobility, and knowledge of the outside world.

## Not Decided

- Whether any family connection, representative role, or mission is retained.
- His relationship with Darkrai and Latios; neither is a settled ownership, bond, or plot mechanism.
- Whether he faces Amber in the final, whether Amber wins, and what either result changes.
- How much he knows about Mega Darkrai, the usurper, Alexander, or Ashura's internal crisis.

Do not make Tobias a Champion-system conspiracy explanation by default. His connection to Ashura, if kept, should support reconnaissance and early contact rather than immediate conquest.
