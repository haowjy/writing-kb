# Arc 12 — Sinnoh: Standard Conference

**Status:** drafting

Sinnoh follows the two-year Alonso/Mystery Dungeon adventurer interval. Amber enters the **Standard Conference**, not the ACE tournament. Tobias also competes in the Standard Conference; whether Amber reaches him in the final, wins the tournament, or loses before that remains open.

## Structural Job

Sinnoh tests the experience Amber earned in the interval against an ordinary public competitive structure. It is also the first sustained place where she repeatedly disrupts Team Galactic's early, hidden preparations rather than confronting its completed end-state plan.

## Conference

- The Standard Conference is distinct from both the U19 Junior Cup and the ACE tournament. Eight recognized Gym Badges qualify a licensed trainer for the Standard path; ACE recognition is not required.
- Tobias is a Standard entrant. Do not label him an ACE competitor.
- Amber's final placement and whether she wins the Standard Conference are open. Do not turn Tobias into an automatic final boss or a foregone Amber victory.

## Team Galactic

Team Galactic is in an early/preparatory hidden phase. Amber repeatedly interferes with operations as she encounters them. The exact operation sequence, how visible Cyrus is, and whether the arc ends in dismantlement are open.

## Tobias

Tobias is an outward-facing Ashuran possibility, not settled character truth. The working possibilities are that he is Alexander's cousin and the usurper's son, crossed the barrier as a representative or scout, and initially does not know Alexander survived. Fame, credentials, mobility, and outside-world knowledge are plausible motives. His Darkrai/Latios relationship, mission, loyalties, and exact family/political position remain open.

See [Tobias](tobias.md) and the [Ashura succession decision](../../decisions/ashura-visit-one-succession-model.md).
