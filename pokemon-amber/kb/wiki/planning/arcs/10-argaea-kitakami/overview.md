# Arc 10 --- Argaea: Kitakami

**Status:** drafting

**Arc:** 10 (Visit 1, Amber age ~16)

Amber and Prince Alexander accidentally enter Argaea, resolve a local Kitakami
crisis, then lose the larger confrontation and escape behind a temporary seal.
The [Argaea saga thread](../../saga-threads/argaea.md) owns the cross-arc frame.

## Setting

- Argaea is a physically and dimensionally sealed region in the ordinary
  Pokemon world, with distinct provinces and continuous internal history.
- The AZ / Mega Darkrai catastrophe sealed it about 3,000 years ago.
- Kitakami is one community within Argaea; travel from elsewhere in Argaea does
  not cross the outer barrier.
- Mew and Amber's trace Mew DNA have no role in access or sealing.

The exact relationship among Argaea's physical pocket, the Mega Dimension, and
its thresholds remains open.

## Visit 1

### Kitakami

Amber and Alexander enter together by accident and spend several days resolving
the Ogerpon / Loyal Three crisis. Amber and Gardevoir discover, or are forced to
discover, Mega Evolution during this stage, before Gardevoir's separation.

### Mad King and Mega Darkrai

After Kitakami, Alexander kills his father because the Mad King has gone mad or
corrupt and must be stopped. Mega Darkrai visibly tears free from the King's
body at death. Amber and Alexander cannot defeat it and retreat.

The being is Mega Darkrai in author truth. Amber has never seen the form and
does not know it exists, so she cannot identify it as Mega Darkrai in the scene.
Its appearance and her description or assumption remain open.

### Escape and Temporary Seal

Cresselia catastrophically exhausts and injures herself holding Mega Darkrai
back during the retreat. Amber and Alexander escape; the exact choreography and
route remain open.

Cresselia then remains inside as the seal's internal counterweight. She directs
Amber and Gardevoir to use Mega Evolution in a sealing action, after which
Gardevoir must stay at or outside the threshold as external support. The patch
buys roughly a few years. Cresselia is not established as dead, and Gardevoir's
long-term state should not be assumed to be a continuous Mega form.

**Result:** the Mad King is dead; Mega Darkrai is loose inside Argaea; Amber and
Alexander escape; Cresselia and Gardevoir hold the temporary seal. The death
creates a power vacuum that may develop into civil war before Visit 2.

## Provisional Adjacent Material

These possibilities must be reconciled with accidental entry before use:

- the Mt. Silver anomaly and dragon gauntlet as the access route;
- Oak's unfinished Mt. Silver mystery and later payoff;
- Gible acquisition in the approach;
- Ursaring's evolution into Ursaluna inside Argaea;
- the Pikachu handoff on the later road home.

The Loyal Three may be former guardians corrupted through Mega Darkrai,
Pecharunt, or Toxic Chains. Hoopa may help navigate an existing distortion but
cannot replace Darkrai as Argaea's cause.
