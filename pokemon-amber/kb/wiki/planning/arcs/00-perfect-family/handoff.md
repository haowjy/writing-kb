## Amber

Same person as before the skip. She wanted to explore the world of Pokemon — childhood dream — and that curiosity got her into trouble.

[ch17]: when Oak mentions a trainer journey, she asks when she can leave.

After four years she still wants that. She belongs at Oak's ranch. School stays thin; she is a loner among classmates.

[Amber](../../../reference/characters/humans/amber-mc.md)

## Household

- Lives with Delia and Ash. Delia is maternal; Amber treats her as a friendlike cohabitant. No required first "Mom." No Stephen-guilt confession in the launch chapter.
- Ash is little-brother-like. Do not recap his recovery.
- Oak is her sponsor and workplace.

## Ditto at journey start

- Already the partner. Ch18 ceremony recognizes that.
- Mellowed, sometimes careless, still a protector. Playful with her.
- Amber treats it as a normal game-Ditto (copy what's in front of you). She finds the form buffer on the road.
- Objects and people are legal copies ([ch3]). Combat buffer is still Pokemon forms.
