# Indigo Champion Succession

**Status:** speculation

The private current direction is **Oak -> Pryce -> Lance**. Oak relinquishes office after the first Kanto secession war; Pryce serves as the postwar/intervening Champion; Lance succeeds Pryce only recently.

This is not yet fixed continuity and need not be named in Chapter 17.5. Exact dates, legal procedure, whether Lance defeated Pryce or succeeded him another way, and Pryce's postwar politics remain open.
