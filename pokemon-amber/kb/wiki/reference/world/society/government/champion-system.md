# Champion/Protector and Indigo Council

**Status:** drafting

The modern Indigo Champion/Protector is the strongest trainer, emergency defender, military symbol, and commander. It is not an ordinary policy-making office.

## Terms and Layers

- **Pokemon League:** global generic system of trainer, Gym, Elite Four, and Champion institutions.
- **Indigo League:** joint Kanto-Johto institution for Champion/Protector and Elite Four authority, defense, Gym and trainer regulation, and major interregional or international functions.
- **Indigo Council:** elected policy-making government created by civic reforms; it handles taxation, infrastructure, education, commerce, and civil law.
- **Kanto regional government:** lower regional civil government.
- **Viridian city government:** municipal government.

Avoid the generic phrase *Indigo government* when **Indigo Council** or **Indigo League administration** is precise.

## Authority and Checks

Ancient Indigo's hereditary Council of Houses left commoners and noncombatants without representation, and its Champion was a warrior-protector within trainer aristocracy. Civic revolutions before Oak's era reduced noble-only rule and the Champion's ordinary policy authority.

Old noble families and Kanto industrial elites retain practical influence through wealth, patronage, Gyms, schools, and candidate funding. Exact Council franchise, constitution, emergency powers, and checks remain open. The settled principle is that battle authority cannot by itself make policy.

## Champion Challenge and the Second Secession

The established route is a lawful Elite Four plus Champion gauntlet. Administration can decide certification, access, scheduling, security, and regulatory questions; it cannot simply falsify matches or compel a Champion to lose.

Giovanni challenges Lance for Champion/Protector because the title could legitimize orderly peaceful Kanto secession. Giovanni uses a conventional legal team and keeps Mewtwo #2 secret. Lance uses classified, government-sponsored Mega Evolution and wins. The Mega can be legal, while its secrecy leaves the fairness contestable.

Giovanni then forcefully secedes Kanto rather than becoming unified Champion/Protector. Lance remains in the office. Whether Kanto later creates a separate Champion/Protector office is open.

## Historical Pressure

Oak's brief title made him a Kanto-separatist symbol; his abandonment of secession and support for Lance's reunification succession shows why the office cannot be treated as unchecked sovereignty.
