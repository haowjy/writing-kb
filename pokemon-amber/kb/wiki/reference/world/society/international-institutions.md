# International Institutions

**Status:** drafting

International cooperation is layered. Ancient service networks, decentralized
regional branches, private and professional field organizations, recent treaty
bodies, and a secret elite compact coexist; no organization governs the whole
international order.

## Institutional Layers

| Layer | Development | Function and limits |
|---|---|---|
| [Joy network](../../organizations/joy-network.md) | Grew from nomadic healers and neutral sanctuaries long before modern treaty institutions | Distributes healing, disaster aid, care knowledge, and safe resting places. Its service mission is transregional but not sovereign. |
| [Jenny network](../../organizations/jenny-network.md) | Grew from protectors associated with Joy healers, caravans, and sanctuaries into regional peacekeeping and police branches | Provides much of the local policing backbone. Its branches cooperate across regions without necessarily forming one unified sovereign organization. |
| Adventurer culture and local guilds | Grew from scattered requests, expeditions, rescues, salvage, mercenary work, and other dangerous fieldwork | Remains a voluntary cultural and professional lane. Independent adventurers, private guilds, and bad actors still exist. |
| [International adventurer federation](../../organizations/adventurer-federation.md) | Developed about ten years before the story as Mystery Dungeon incidents worsened | Pools maps, reports, requests, expeditions, rescue support, warnings, equipment, and cross-border logistics. Its exact formal name and credential model remain open. |
| [International Police](../../organizations/international-police.md) | Recently created by treaty with Jenny branches central to its formation | Investigates cross-border crimes and multi-jurisdictional incidents. Its authority depends on treaties and host-region cooperation. |
| [Project X](../../organizations/project-x.md) | Recent secret compact among selected rulers and other established elites | Shares hoarded combat and exceptional-evolution knowledge to strengthen participating powers. It does not distribute those benefits publicly. |

The older networks did not disappear when newer bodies formed. Pokemon Centers
can simultaneously be Joy service sites, local quest-board hosts, and logistics
nodes for the adventurer federation without being controlled by that
federation or the International Police.

## Other Transregional Power

The world's [old noble clans and houses](clan-system.md) are a separate major
power bloc. They may constitute a shared social and political class or a loose
alignment of ancient houses, but no unified worldwide organization is settled.
Their interests are broader than [Project X](../../organizations/project-x.md):
the compact selects some elites without representing the old houses as a
whole.

This bloc remains distinct from Giovanni's Kanto-centered politics, the modern
institutions above, and sealed Argaea.

## Mandate Boundaries

| Institution | Primary mandate |
|---|---|
| Joy network | Heal and protect Pokemon, teach care, support disaster response, and maintain neutral service sites. |
| Adventurers and their federation | Explore, perform contracted fieldwork, conduct expeditions and rescues, and pool operational knowledge. |
| International Police | Investigate crimes, pursue suspects, and coordinate evidence across jurisdictions. |
| [Rangers](../../organizations/ranger-union.md) | Protect ecosystems and the public through route closures, wild-Pokemon response, rescue, and hazard management. |
| Project X | Strengthen the military and combat capacity of participating rulers and elites. |

Overlap creates cooperation, not interchangeable authority. A Dungeon outbreak
may involve adventurers mapping and entering the site, Rangers closing routes
and protecting habitats, local Jenny branches maintaining order, and the
International Police investigating trafficking or sabotage. None gains the
others' mandate merely by joining the same operation.

## Political Contrast

- The Joy network carries healing wherever it is needed.
- The adventurer federation pools field knowledge and operations.
- The International Police crosses borders so that borders do not shelter
  criminals, but only through agreed jurisdiction.
- Project X embraces international cooperation while restricting its power and
  benefits to rulers, lineages, officeholders, and other accepted elites.

## Planning-Grade Chronology

These are relative eras, not exact canon dates.

1. **Centuries or longer ago:** nomadic Joy healers travel among isolated
   settlements with associated Jenny protectors.
2. **As routes and cities stabilize:** sanctuaries become clinics and Pokemon
   Centers; Jenny branches expand into peacekeeping and local policing.
3. **For generations:** scattered request networks and rough local adventurer
   guilds support exploration, rescue, recovery, and dangerous wild work.
4. **About ten years before the story:** reputable guilds, Joy/Pokemon Center
   networks, Rangers, and governments establish a newer international
   adventurer body in response to worsening Mystery Dungeon incidents.
5. **Within recent years or the past decade:** Jenny branches help establish
   the International Police by treaty.
6. **Recently:** Project X forms, but its exact founding date and ownership
   remain open.
