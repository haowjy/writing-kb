# International Institutions

**Status:** drafting

International cooperation is layered; no body governs the whole international
order.

| Institution | Scope |
|---|---|
| [Joy network](../../organizations/joy-network.md) | Ancient transregional healing, disaster aid, care knowledge, and Pokemon Center service. |
| [Jenny network](../../organizations/jenny-network.md) | Decentralized regional protector and police branches with shared history. |
| [Adventurer federation](../../organizations/adventurer-federation.md) | A roughly decade-old coordinator for Mystery Dungeon reports, expeditions, rescues, and cross-border logistics. |
| [International Police](../../organizations/international-police.md) | A recent treaty body for cross-border crime and multi-jurisdictional investigations. |
| [Ranger Union](../../organizations/ranger-union.md) | Ecosystem protection, route closures, wild-Pokemon response, rescue, and wilderness safety. |
| [Project X](../../organizations/project-x.md) | A secret compact that strengthens participating rulers' and elites' military capacity. |

The older Joy, Jenny, adventurer, and quest-board networks remain in use beneath
newer institutions. Their mandates can overlap during one incident without
becoming interchangeable.

The [old noble houses](clan-system.md) are a separate, loose transregional
political class. Project X selects some elites without representing that class
as a whole.
