# Paldea: Knighthood and the Journey of Glory

Paldea's journey tradition never democratized. Kanto and Paldea both began as
noble, class-bound institutions, but Kanto opened its trainer/badge journey to
the public after the Pokeball revolution (see [trainer cultural
history](../systems/trainers/cultural-history.md)); Paldea largely did not. The
result is a region where travel, discovery, and glory remain entangled with
nobility, knighthood, and patronage --- and where a commoner like [Alonso
Quijano](../../characters/humans/alonso-quijano.md) has no public circuit to
join.

## The Treasure Hunt / Journey of Glory

Paldea's traditional journey --- the Treasure Hunt, also called the Journey of
Glory --- is primarily an **elite rite for noble youths and
knight-apprentices**, not a broad public institution like Kanto's badge
journey. Participants travel to:

- serve the country and kingdom,
- make discoveries,
- win glory and build a name,
- form patronage connections,
- return seeking legal office or recognition.

There is no public badge circuit in Paldea. Paldeans raised outside the elite
rite may have no framework for what Kanto badges gate or signal.

## Knighthood as Legal Office

Paldean knighthood is an actual **legally recognized office**, not a courtesy
title or aesthetic.

- Historically, knights were overwhelmingly tied to nobility.
- Nonnoble upward mobility remains possible through **exceptional service and
  official recognition** --- the Spanish/Iberian pattern of a commoner raised
  for deeds. It is rare, not closed.
- **Fame is leverage, not status.** Renown helps secure commissions, patrons,
  tournament invitations, and eventually knighthood or (rarely) ennoblement,
  but fame alone does not automatically confer rank.

## Access, Tournaments, and Segregation

Paldea retains meaningful noble/commoner segregation in its adventuring life:

- Prestigious tournaments are largely **invitation-based**.
- Valuable adventuring areas --- Crown, noble, and knightly lands, ruins, and
  Mystery Dungeons --- often carry explicit noble/knight restrictions or
  sponsorship barriers.
- Prestigious expeditions run through invitation networks rather than open
  application.

A talented commoner can be stronger than a knight and still be locked out of
the places and events where names are made. This is the structural pressure
that pushes Alonso toward Kanto's newly appearing, not-yet-claimed Mystery
Dungeons.

## Charcadet, Pokemon of Knighthood

Charcadet is culturally **the Pokemon of knighthood and old nobility** in
Paldea:

- fiery fighting spirit and exceptional flame control,
- ancient bonds with humans; historically accompanied nobles and knights,
- its evolution is shaped by **inherited armor** bearing auspicious wishes or
  malicious will --- the honor/malevolence split behind Armarouge and
  Ceruledge (see [Charcadet → Ceruledge](../../../planning/saga-threads/charcadet-ceruledge.md)).

A Charcadet bond suggests knightly potential but does not legally make anyone
a knight. Auspicious Armor can be an heirloom or knightly-order legacy
entrusted to a worthy Charcadet; the exact legal custody of such armor is
deliberately left open.
