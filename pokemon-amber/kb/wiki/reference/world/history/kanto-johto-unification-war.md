# Kanto Secession War and Indigo Reunification

**Status:** drafting

Kanto and Johto were already regions within unified Indigo. The first conflict was Kanto's failed secession from that shared state, not a war that created the League or joined foreign countries.

## Government Before and After Reform

Ancient Indigo governance was a hereditary Council of Houses and nobles. Commoners and noncombatants lacked representation; the Champion was a warrior-protector, perhaps first among the trainer aristocracy.

Civic revolutions before Oak became Champion replaced or reduced noble-only rule with an elected **Indigo Council** that represents citizens and noncombatants, and reduced the Champion's ordinary policy authority. Old noble families and Kanto industrial elites still exercise practical influence through wealth, patronage, Gyms, schools, and candidate funding.

The modern Champion/Protector is the strongest trainer, emergency defender, military symbol and commander, not the ordinary policy-maker. The Indigo Council handles taxation, infrastructure, education, commerce, and civil law. Exact constitutional checks and franchise remain open.

## Regional Balance

Johto retained older clans and institutions; Kanto developed the stronger industrial and scientific base. Both were interdependent. Nationalist claims that Johto simply lived off Kanto wealth erase Johto's institutional, ecological, and breeding contributions; they are political rhetoric, not neutral fact.

## Oak and the Secession

Kanto-born Samuel Oak became unified Indigo Champion/Protector. His victory energized Kanto separatists. Under elite and public pressure, Oak briefly supported and fought for Kanto secession, believing force could bring a short war and negotiation. He bears real culpability.

When bloodshed escalated, Oak sought negotiation, abandoned the secession, relinquished the title, and supported Lance as reunification Protector. The exact war duration, title-succession procedure, and Mt. Silver's role remain open.

## Contested Memory

- **Kanto nationalist memory:** Oak proved Kanto could lead, then betrayed independence.
- **Johto memory:** Oak participated in an illegal Kanto secession.
- **Moderate view:** Oak made a grave choice, recognized it, and prevented worse harm.

These are interpretations of shared history, not interchangeable facts.

## Lasting Settlement

Reunification restored the shared Indigo order. Kanto and Johto retain regional governments and institutions beneath the Indigo Council and League administration; Viridian city government is a lower municipal layer. The old war's infrastructure and grievance later feed Team Rocket.

## Open Questions

- Exact war duration.
- Oak's title relinquishment and Lance's succession.
- Mt. Silver's precise role, timing, and public memory.
- Indigo Council franchise, constitution, and emergency checks.
