# Adventurer System

**Status:** drafting

**Adventurer** is a cultural and professional identity for people centered on
wilderness exploration, expeditions, rescue, recovery, salvage, and discovery.
It is not a trainer license, quest-board class, or source of legal authority.
Private guilds may set membership or expedition requirements, but the legal
baseline for Pokemon handling and Center contracts remains the trainer license.

## Regional Meaning

Kanto's dominant ideal is the badge journey and League competition. Paldea's is
exploration, the Treasure Hunt, and Area Zero. This difference makes Alonso's
self-description as an adventurer feel foreign in Kanto without requiring a
separate legal profession.

[Alonso Quijano](../../../characters/humans/alonso-quijano.md) brings the
Paldean ideal into Kanto. He pursues the title of Greatest Adventurer, travels
alone with his Pokemon, and values discovery over legal office. Don Q, a shabby
storyteller, made that ideal feel possible. Alonso sincerely becomes what Don Q
only performed. Alonso's exact family origin remains open.

## Travel and Work

Long-distance adventuring exists but remains unusual because off-route Pokemon,
unmapped terrain, supplies, and cross-region law make expeditions dangerous and
expensive. Ships, aviation, Centers, communications, mapping, Pokeballs, and
Repel make it increasingly possible. Reports of numerous Mystery Dungeon
discoveries draw Alonso from Paldea to Kanto; the journey consumes nearly all
his money, leaving him broke when he arrives.

Adventurers often use Pokemon Center [quest
boards](quest-system.md), but the board classifies jobs rather than identities.
Some adventurers are privately funded; some quest specialists do not consider
themselves adventurers. Mystery Dungeon work intensifies the culture without
creating an Adventurer or Explorer license.

The newer [adventurer federation](../../../organizations/adventurer-federation.md)
coordinates Dungeon operations across borders without replacing older local
guilds, independent crews, or the cultural identity.

## Guilds and Reputation

Guilds and fellowships provide mutual aid, equipment, reputation, and expedition
logistics. The Horizons Explorers are one such private fellowship and one of
Alonso's models, not a universal institution.

The label does not guarantee competence or ethics. Adventurers may pursue
salvage, rare Pokemon, or location data with flexible ethics, and private crews
can be predatory without becoming Team Rocket. Alonso responds when someone is
hurt rather than becoming an anti-corruption spokesman; Amber is more likely to
notice the wider pattern.

Use lowercase **explorer** descriptively. **Adventurer** is the umbrella
identity; named groups may use *Explorer*, but there is no separate worldwide
Explorer profession.
