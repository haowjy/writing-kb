# Mystery Dungeons

**Status:** drafting

Mystery Dungeons are ancient natural phenomena: unstable wilderness zones
where geography, Pokemon behavior, energy, and spatial logic cease behaving
normally.

Common traits include:

- interiors larger than their exteriors;
- looping forests and routes;
- ruins that rearrange themselves;
- isolated storms or distorted habitats;
- local effects from legendary residue, ecological feedback, or dimensional
  stress.

They are uncommon, not secret. Rangers, Pokemon Centers, experienced trainers,
and adventurers have warnings and procedures for them. Their underlying science
remains disputed.

Mystery Dungeons are not portals. Ultra Wormholes connect different worlds;
Dungeons distort places within the Pokemon world.

## Systemic Destabilization

Human activity does not create Mystery Dungeons. It destabilizes existing sites
and contributes to worsening incidents. The current outbreak model allows an
unstable site to overflow or "break": distorted terrain, weather or habitat
effects, displaced or aggressive Pokemon, and looping geography may escape its
known boundary and threaten ordinary routes or settlements. These exact
dungeon-break effects remain working direction rather than fixed cosmology.

Possible pressures include ecological destruction, intensive energy
extraction, high-energy experiments, Ultra Wormhole research, disruption of
legendary Pokemon, and wartime production. Their relative importance remains
open. The crisis is cumulative rather than the work of one mastermind or evil
Pokemon.

Argaea is a sealed pocket region with its own mechanism. Laqua is not the
source of Mystery Dungeons or dimensional essence.

Whether the apparent spread means literal physical expansion or new land is
unresolved. It may instead reflect destabilization of known Dungeons plus the
discovery of previously unknown sites. The committed operational direction is
outbreak and containment, not either cosmological explanation.

## Containment

Expeditions may colloquially say they "defeated" a Dungeon. In practice, they
contain outbreaks, rescue victims, remove aggravating factors, and force
distortions or displaced Pokemon back from inhabited terrain. Whether any
Dungeon can be permanently destroyed is unknown.

The newer [adventurer federation](../../organizations/adventurer-federation.md)
exists partly to coordinate this pushback across regions. Rangers still own
route closures, ecosystem response, and public safety; federation expeditions
own exploration and contracted field operations.

## Story Use

Mystery Dungeons support exploration, rescue work, dangerous off-route travel,
and encounters with Pokemon outside their expected habitats. They also explain
why game knowledge does not equal wilderness expertise.

Alonso treats them as maps, rumors, expeditions, and proof of his adventurer
ideal. Amber gradually gains field knowledge and navigation experience through
them; whether she claims the adventurer identity remains open. Argaea later
tests her inside a sealed realm, while Ultra Space carries the exploration
thread into other worlds.

The introduction remains gradual. Chapters 20-21 use a guarded,
dungeon-adjacent Route 1 cave without explaining the full phenomenon. Explicit
Dungeon exploration comes later through Alonso, quests, and Ranger procedures.

## Institutions and Knowledge

| Group | What they know |
|---|---|
| Civilians | Warnings, disappearances, disasters, and rescue reports. |
| New trainers | Basic precautions and posted closures. |
| Quest-taking trainers | Hazard grades, proof requirements, and basic Ranger procedure. |
| Rangers | Monitoring, closures, rescue protocols, and ecological risk. |
| Adventurers | Maps, field lore, survival habits, and expedition records. |
| Researchers | Energy signatures, ecological patterns, history, and competing theories. |
| Team Rocket | Internal evidence that its activity worsens the crisis. |

Readers should infer Rocket's culpability before recovered scientific records
confirm what Giovanni knew. The investigator, location, and timing remain open.

## Quest Integration

Mystery Dungeon work uses the existing [Quest System & Job
Boards](../systems/trainers/quest-system.md):

- `[Rescue]` lost trainers or stranded Pokemon;
- `[Item]` missing gear, samples, or cargo;
- `[Investigation]` shifting terrain or unusual energy;
- `[Defeat]` aggressive Pokemon leaving a Dungeon;
- `[Escort]` protection for a research team.

No universal Explorer or Adventurer license is needed. Rangers manage public
safety; adventurers map and enter dangerous sites; the federation connects
requests, reports, logistics, and rescue capacity across borders.

## Constraints

- Route 1 remains dungeon-adjacent rather than becoming a full Dungeon arc.
- Formal status comes from trainer licensing, not an Explorer profession.
- Procedures and research should make Dungeons legible without solving their
  mystery.
- Mystery Dungeons, Ultra Wormholes, Argaea, and Laqua remain distinct.
