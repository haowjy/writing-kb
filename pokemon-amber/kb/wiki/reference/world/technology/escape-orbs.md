# Escape Orbs

Escape Orbs are ordinary safety equipment for a deliberate entry into a known,
regulated Mystery Dungeon. A working Orb costs **₽300**, compared with **₽200**
for a standard Pokeball; it is a meaningful recurring expedition cost rather
than a rare plot device.

## Entry Safety Rule

Rangers treat carrying an Escape Orb as a general safety requirement at
regulated Dungeon entrances and known Dungeon outskirts. They may inspect it
alongside Trainer ID and active-custody compliance. Deliberately entering
without one is a civil safety violation, comparable to a seat-belt fine; it is
not automatically a serious crime.

This rule applies to planned entry. Accidental or forced entry, including the
Route 1 collapse that leads into the Ch. 23--24 Dungeon, is not a violation.

## Unsettled Mechanics

The following are useful working possibilities but are **not settled canon**:

- whether an Orb is single-use;
- who it extracts (one person, a party, or everyone in a radius);
- required backups or party quantity;
- failure modes in unstable, sealed, or otherwise exceptional Dungeons.

Do not infer an explorer license from the equipment rule. Existing trainer
licensing, local Dungeon access rules, and Ranger authority are the current
basis for enforcement; separate explorer qualifications or hazard ratings
remain open.
