# Pokemon Augmentation Technologies

These are three distinct technologies. Do not treat their shared interest in Pokemon strength as a shared origin, mechanism, or safety profile.

> **Status and provenance:** This page records settled planning direction, not published canon. Dr. Kihara, the serum's now-dead creator, is named on the page [ch6]. The serum's formal research name, intended purpose, and full history remain open.

## X Attack: regulated temporary enhancement

X Attack is a Viridian Gym / Giovanni-company development, tested in controlled Gym trials. It provides temporary strength augmentation while normal fainting remains intact. It is a regulated public-facing technology, not a forced-evolution treatment.

Silph may manufacture or distribute X Attack under license, including regional production, but does not originate it. See [Viridian Gym](../../places/kanto/viridian-gym.md).

## Experimental strength-augmentation serum: profound and unsafe

Dr. Kihara, now dead, created an experimental serum with Fuji as a close collaborator. It can produce a profound but unpredictable increase in strength. Known or observed risks include grotesque deformation, suppression or override of normal fainting, and death on defeat rather than fainting. Partial evolutionary anatomy is an observed side effect; it does not establish the serum's intended purpose or classify it as forced evolution.

Fuji did not create the serum; Kihara gave him a prototype vial [ch6]. After the Pallet attack the vial's last drops are with Oak, who sends a sample to the League [ch17.5]. It is not X Attack. See [Dr. Fuji](../../characters/humans/dr-fuji.md).

## Sebastian's radio-wave forced evolution: a separate Rocket line

> [!FLAG] **Needs human review**: in ch6 Sebastian reports that "the forced
> evolution serum research was largely preserved"; this section makes his line
> radio-wave, not a serum. Flagged 2026-11-10.

Sebastian's Rocket research remotely induces evolution through radio waves. It is a separate forced-evolution research line, not the experimental serum and not X Attack. Its precise development status, ownership, and operational limits remain open.
