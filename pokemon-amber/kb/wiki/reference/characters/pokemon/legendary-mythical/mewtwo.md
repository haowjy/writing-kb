# Mewtwo

Created by Dr. Fuji under Team Rocket's Project Chimera — a clone of Mew, weaponized. The "successful" product of the same lab that produced Ditto and Amber.

## Origin

Fuji's primary deliverable to Team Rocket: clone Mew into a controllable weapon. Mewtwo is the result. For Fuji personally, Mewtwo was the stepping stone — the proof of concept that would let him perfect the techniques needed to resurrect Amber. Mewtwo was never the goal. Amber was.

Mewtwo carries human DNA admixture as part of the creation process. This is why it holds grudges, processes betrayal the way a person does rather than the way a Pokemon does, and hesitates around "Amber" rather than attacking.

## Connection to Amber

Both are Fuji's creations. Mewtwo from Mew directly; Amber from a human clone with trace Mew DNA. They share an origin in the same lab, the same scientist, the same source material at a remove.

Mewtwo's hesitation to kill the scientists at the moment of awakening is attributed to the familiar presence of "Amber" — the clone he had been exposed to in the lab. Whether that recognition extends to the current Amber (an isekai'd soul in that body, not Fuji's daughter) is an open question.

## Story Role

Off-screen variable for most of the saga. Contact events should be rare and consequential — not a recurring presence, but a region-scale force that exists in the background.

Current long-term direction distinguishes two Mewtwo. The first escaped Cinnabar and eventually corresponds to the female-coded/voiced Mewtwo of the Genesect crisis. Amber meets her again in a future Unova / New Tork City / Pokemon Hills arc, where Amber helps her access or stabilize Mega Mewtwo Y to defend against Genesect. The Route 1 psychic-reactive ore thread is the long physical setup for that Mewtwonite Y payoff.

The full Mewtwo thread — its relationship to Amber, Ditto, Fuji's legacy, and the Mew connection — is the spine of the saga. Needs its own planning session.

## Known Facts

- Holds grudges; interprets threats through a human cognitive frame
- Recognizes "Amber" — exhibits protective/curious behavior rather than aggression
- Escaped the Cinnabar lab at the moment of awakening (ch1)
- Whereabouts during Amber's journey: largely unknown until the future Unova / Genesect crisis

## Mewtwo #2: The Corrected Version

### The Lesson Giovanni Learned

Mewtwo #1 woke up, asked "who am I," was told "you are an experiment," and destroyed the lab. The scientists' mistake wasn't losing control of the weapon. The mistake was treating it like a weapon. A being that powerful will always break chains. Giovanni understood this immediately.

In [Ch 6](https://github.com/haowjy/pokemon-amber/blob/main/story/ch6/chapter6.md), when Fuji explains that Mewtwo thinks like a human — ambitions, desires, grudges — Giovanni responds: *"Then it can understand reason."* Not concern. Opportunity. A being that thinks like a human can be raised, persuaded, and made to *belong*.

### Creation & Raising

Fuji creates a second Mewtwo from remaining Mew DNA samples, alongside new starter clones (Charmandertwo, Bulbasaurtwo, Squirtletwo) and a second Ambertwo — a human clone, genuine blank slate, no isekai consciousness.

Giovanni raises them as a family:
- **Giovanni** as father figure to the Pokemon clones — present from the moment of Mewtwo #2's consciousness
- **Fuji** raising new Ambertwo as his daughter (his motivation for compliance — another chance at the daughter he's spent 15 years trying to resurrect)
- **The clones grow up together as siblings** — Mewtwo #2, the three starters, and new Ambertwo

The clones are accelerated but the project takes years, running in the background during Amber's journey. Giovanni is patient because he's building a family, not a weapon, and families take time.

### Why Mewtwo #2 Is Loyal

Not mind control. Not indoctrination. **Family.**

Mewtwo #2 fights for Giovanni the way any child fights for their parent. The worldview — Kanto was wronged, the old system is corrupt, we're building something better — comes through the relationship, absorbed the way children absorb their parents' beliefs. Through dinner-table conversation, through watching how Giovanni moves through the world, through growing up inside the cause.

Giovanni provides what Mewtwo #1 never had: identity, purpose, respect, and genuine relationship. Mewtwo #2 isn't a slave. It's a true believer. And true belief is harder to break than any chain.

### Thematic Role

Mewtwo #1 and #2 are the same question answered two ways:
- **#1:** Created as a weapon, treated as a product → rebellion, autonomy, refusal to be defined by its creator's purpose
- **#2:** Raised as a child, given identity and family → genuine loyalty, belief in the cause, fighting for the only world it's ever known

The endgame question isn't "can Amber beat Mewtwo #2 in a fight." It's "can Mewtwo #2 be shown what its family actually is — and would seeing it change anything?" Some people never see it. Some people defend their parents forever.
