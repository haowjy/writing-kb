# Pokemon Character Index

Individual Pokemon characters are organized by their durable narrative identity, not necessarily by where they first appear. Species ecology and generic species information belong in world reference rather than this character collection.

## Organization

| Collection | Use |
|---|---|
| [Amber's team](amber-team/index.md) | Pokemon confirmed or firmly planned to belong to Amber |
| [Other trainers](other-trainers/index.md) | Pokemon primarily defined through another trainer |
| [Independent](independent/index.md) | Wild, released, unaffiliated, or individually recurring Pokemon |
| [Legendary and mythical](legendary-mythical/index.md) | Individually significant legendary and mythical Pokemon |
| [Organizational](organizational/index.md) | Pokemon primarily serving an organization rather than one trainer |

## Classification Precedence

When an individual fits several categories, use the first stable identity that applies:

1. Amber's confirmed future team.
2. Another trainer's established team.
3. Legendary or mythical identity.
4. Organizational affiliation.
5. Independent or wild.

Use the Pokemon's durable story identity rather than its temporary custody. Caterpie belongs under Amber's team despite beginning wild; mother Ursaring remains independent despite Rocket captivity; Mewtwo remains legendary/mythical despite Rocket creation and control.

Amber's broader future roster is tracked in [roster planning](../../../planning/rosters/amber-team.md). Emotional arcs between Amber and her Pokemon are tracked in [Amber's team arcs](../../../planning/team-arcs/amber/index.md).
