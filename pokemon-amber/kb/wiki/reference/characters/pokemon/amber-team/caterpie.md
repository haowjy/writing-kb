# Caterpie / Butterfree

Amber's second Pokemon and first successful wild capture. Caterpie is physically weak, afraid of direct combat, and unusually precise with silk. It survives by avoiding contests it expects to lose.

## Acquisition

Planned for [ch19]. Caterpie lowers itself into Amber's backpack on a silk thread to steal food. Ditto throws it away from the supplies; Amber apologizes, approaches with a Potion, then backs off when Caterpie retreats.

Amber leaves a separate bowl beneath the tree. Caterpie returns, eats, and accepts Amber's offer of regular food by pressing the Pokeball button itself. The capture requires no battle. Chapter 19 ends with Amber raising the occupied ball and celebrating with Ditto.

## Personality

- Openly afraid and unwilling to initiate direct combat.
- Looks for cover and escape routes before confronting danger.
- Freezes, shrinks, hides, or retreats rather than pretending to be strong.
- Food-motivated enough to take calculated risks.
- Proud of *String Shot*, the one technique it trusts.
- Ashamed that fear and physical weakness left it behind its peers.
- Honest about disliking battle once Amber understands its refusal.

Caterpie has no hidden power that makes its fear arbitrary. Its eventual courage consists of acting while afraid.

## Survival History

Leaves kept Caterpie alive, but larger peers claimed scarce berries and other rich food. Caterpie learned to wait above feeding areas, lower itself silently on silk, take what it could, and retreat through the branches before discovery. Repetition produced unusually careful *String Shot* placement.

Its peers evolved into Metapod and then Butterfree. Caterpie remained beneath the canopy and watched them leave.

## Capabilities

- ***String Shot:*** Caterpie's practiced technique. Its precision exceeds its confidence in battle.
- ***Tackle:*** Physically possible, but feared and poorly suited to its small body.
