# Domino

Team Rocket operative. Appears in [Ch 6](https://github.com/haowjy/pokemon-amber/blob/main/story/ch6/chapter6.md) as the source of the
Delia surveillance dossier; her reports resurface in
[Ch 13](https://github.com/haowjy/pokemon-amber/blob/main/story/ch13/chapter13.md) spread across the log where Fuji waits to kill Stephen.

## Timeline Placement --- Read This First

**This is years before Domino exists in canon.** In *Mewtwo Returns* she is an Elite
Officer, alias 009 and the Black Tulip, taking orders directly from Giovanni. None of
that is true yet.

She is not 009. She does not have Giovanni's direct line. Nobody calls her the Black
Tulip. **Do not "correct" her seniority against Bulbapedia** --- the gap is deliberate.

What she does have is the talent. The competence that eventually makes her an Elite
Officer is fully present; the rank is not.

**She gets there.** By the time canon opens she is the Elite Officer of *Mewtwo Returns*,
fully formed. This story covers the climb, so every appearance should leave her further
up than it found her. Nothing about her arc is tragic or ironic --- she wants the job and
she earns it.

## Ch 6 Is Her Come-Up

She freelances. Odd jobs for various important figures across the Rocket organization,
building a client list and a reputation one favor at a time. Fuji hired her to find
Delia, and he happens to be a senior scientist with Giovanni's ear --- which makes the
job worth doing well.

She finished it, then had to track him down to deliver. Gideon passes the message along:
*"Agent Domino was asking about you last night. Said she has something you'll find
interesting. She's at the Rose Heights Apartments, Unit 742."*

**Note what she didn't do.** She could have handed the folder to Gideon and been done.
Instead a senior scientist crosses the city at night and comes to her apartment. She
turned a delivery into a meeting, and everything she does once he's inside is working
the client:

- **She stages it as a social occasion.** Two wine glasses, a bottle uncorked for herself.
  He came for a folder; she is trying to make it a relationship.
- **She sells the product before handing it over.** *"She held it just out of reach, smile
  sharpening. 'New address, a few photos, some documents. Just... don't be too shocked,
  okay?'"* She makes him want it.
- **She demonstrates she read the file.** *"It's natural, you know. For her to want
  companionship after all this time."* That is not comfort. It is a junior operative
  proving to a client that she understood the emotional stakes of the job, and enjoying
  the leverage a little.

The sparse apartment is tradecraft, not poverty --- no personal items, nothing that says
who she is, exercise equipment in the corner. She keeps herself ready.

## Canon Basis

Three references, all Bond:

| Alias | Origin |
|---|---|
| Domino | Domino Vitali, the Bond girl of *Thunderball* (1961 novel); Dominique "Domino" Derval in the 1965 film |
| 009 | MI6 double-0 codes |
| The Black Tulip | the Alexandre Dumas novel |

She is built as a Bond girl *and* a double-0. The glamour is load-bearing, not decoration,
and it should read as a professional asset she deploys on purpose. See [Bulbapedia][bulba].

Canon traits that are already true of her here: quick temper held in check when the job
needs it, a skilled actress who can hold a friendly persona indefinitely, and real
contempt for operatives who embarrass the organization. Canon traits that are *not* yet
true: elite rank, Giovanni's trust, the authority to discipline anyone.

## Writing Her

**She notices everything and shows nothing.** Reading people is the skill that eventually
makes her 009. In Ch 6 she clocks Fuji reversing himself on a drink --- of course she
does --- and it reaches her face not at all.

It also means nothing to her. She has no access to the twelve sober years, so what she
files is *"Fuji drinks."* That is the whole reason the moment can sit in the chapter
without spoiling itself: the one person watching is sharp enough to see it and missing
the context that would make it matter. See [Dr. Fuji --- The Alcohol Timeline][fuji-alcohol].

**Do not write her as a handler.** She is not managing Fuji, manipulating him toward
alcohol, or running a long game on him. She is ambitious, she did good work, and she
wants the credit.

**Her POV is unavailable.** Ch 6 is close third on Fuji. Everything about her arrives
through the eye of a man who is not looking at her, which is its own characterization of
him.
