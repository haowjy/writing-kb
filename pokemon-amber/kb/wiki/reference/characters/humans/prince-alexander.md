# Prince Alexander

**Status:** drafting

Prince Alexander of Ashura is Ashura's prince and Amber's companion on both visits. He is
not a later Kanto conqueror, liberation-that-curdles mirror, or endgame
antagonist; that role duplicated Giovanni's.

## Current Direction

During Kanto, Alexander travels alone on a route separate from Amber, Kyle, and
Alonso. He intersects with the core trio periodically as a mysterious rival;
he is not a fourth party member. His identity as Prince Alexander of Ashura is
initially concealed or unknown.

Alexander escaped when Ashura's failing dimensional seal opened a temporary
breach. His passage into the ordinary Pokemon world is itself evidence that the
barrier is deteriorating and constitutes Ashura's first outside contact in
roughly three thousand years. The exact physical route and immediate event that
opened that particular breach remain open; do not reopen whether he escaped
through the failing seal.

What makes the Kanto rivalry concrete remains open.

His solitude should let him develop as an independent trajectory rather than a
companion waiting for Amber's plot.

Alexander comes from Ashura's continuously developing society, not 3,000 years
of personal stasis. The timing and consequence of his escape, his first reunion
with Amber, and any return to Ashura are open, because Ashura's succession
model is not chosen; see [Ashura](../../../planning/saga-threads/ashura.md).

An Ashuran unifier, dispossessed heir, or returning claimant role remains
possible, but Alexander's political outcome is open.

## Open

- the breach's exact physical route, immediate trigger, and how much Alexander understood before crossing;
- age, upbringing, political position, and prior knowledge of the King,
  Cresselia, Mega Darkrai, and the barrier;
- personality, voice, and temperament;
- Kanto team and the practical domain of his rivalry with Amber;
- his role in the first Ashura visit and anything after it.
