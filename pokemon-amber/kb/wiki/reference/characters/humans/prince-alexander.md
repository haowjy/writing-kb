# Prince Alexander

**Status:** drafting

Prince Alexander is Argaea's prince and Amber's companion on both visits. He is
not a later Kanto conqueror, liberation-that-curdles mirror, or endgame
antagonist; that role duplicated Giovanni's.

## Current Direction

Alexander comes from Argaea's continuously developing society, not 3,000 years
of personal stasis. He and Amber enter Argaea together by accident during Visit
1. After Kitakami, he kills his mad or corrupted father; Mega Darkrai escapes
the King's body, and Cresselia holds it back while Alexander and Amber retreat.

By Visit 2, Alexander has the resolve to return deliberately with Amber and a
recruited group. Their goals are to replace the failing seal, free Gardevoir,
and resolve the larger crisis.

An internal Argaean unifier role remains possible if the provisional civil-war
direction survives planning. This does not commit Alexander to conquering,
reclaiming the throne, or any political outcome.

## Open

- his first escape, time outside, and any responsibility for the breach;

  Cresselia, Mega Darkrai, and the barrier;
- personality, voice, and temperament;
- exact role in Visit 1's confrontation and after Visit 2.
