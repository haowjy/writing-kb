# Lance

**Status:** drafting

Lance is the Blackthorn-associated Johto trainer who succeeds Oak as **unified Indigo Champion** after the Kanto secession war. His office represents reunification and the continuing legitimacy of the shared Indigo League, not a separate Johto championship imposed on Kanto.

Kanto nationalists often treat Lance as proof that Oak surrendered Kanto leadership to Johto clan power. Johto and moderate accounts instead see his succession as part of restoring lawful Indigo authority after an illegal secession. The exact legal procedure of the succession remains open.

Giovanni later defeats Lance through the lawful Indigo Elite Four and Champion gauntlet. Lance is not required to lose or falsify the match; Giovanni's victory is real, which is why it carries such dangerous legitimacy.

## Open Questions

- Lance's exact relationship to Blackthorn institutions and clans.
- His personal view of Oak, Kanto nationalism, and Giovanni's modernization.
- The institutional role he plays after losing the Championship.
