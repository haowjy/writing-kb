# Jenny Network

**Status:** drafting

The Jenny network consists of decentralized regional protector and police
branches. Jennys historically guarded Joy healers, caravans, sanctuaries, and
service routes; their work grew into local peacekeeping and policing.

The branches retain local jurisdiction while sharing traditions and
cross-border ties. They helped found the [International
Police](international-police.md) and provide much of its local backbone.

Whether Jennys began within the Joy clan or as a separate allied protector clan
remains open.
