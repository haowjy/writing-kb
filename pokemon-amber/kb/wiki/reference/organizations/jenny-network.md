# Jenny Network

**Status:** drafting

The Jenny network is a family of decentralized regional protector and police
branches. Shared history and professional ties make it transregional, but it
is not necessarily one perfectly unified sovereign organization.

## History and Development

Jennys historically served as protectors associated with Joy healers,
sanctuaries, and caravans. Their work widened into general peacekeeping as
settlements grew and then into local policing as cities and regional
governments formed.

The established branches recently helped found the [International
Police](international-police.md). They now provide much of its local
institutional backbone while retaining their ordinary regional and municipal
law-enforcement roles.

## Structure

- Regional Jenny branches answer to their own local legal and political
  systems.
- Branches share traditions, relationships, and operational familiarity across
  borders.
- International cooperation does not erase regional jurisdiction or turn every
  Jenny officer into an International Police agent.

## Open Origin Decision

Two origins remain possible, and neither is canonized:

1. Jennys were once a protector branch within the Joy clan and later became
   institutionally distinct.
2. Jennys were always a separate allied protector clan serving alongside Joy
   healers and sanctuaries.
