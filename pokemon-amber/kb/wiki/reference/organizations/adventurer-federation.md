# Adventurer Federation

**Status:** drafting

About ten years before the story, worsening Mystery Dungeon incidents led
reputable guilds, Joy/Pokemon Center networks, Rangers, and governments to form
an international coordinating body. **Adventurer Federation** and
**International Adventurers Guild** are working descriptions; its formal name
is open.

It pools Dungeon requests, maps, reports, warnings, expeditions, rescue support,
equipment, and cross-border logistics. It uses the older Pokemon Center [quest
boards](../world/systems/trainers/quest-system.md) rather than replacing them.

The federation does not absorb older [adventurer
culture](../world/systems/trainers/adventurer-system.md), private guilds, or
independent crews. Adventurer remains a cultural identity, while trainer
licensing remains the legal credential for handling Pokemon and accepting
Center contracts.

Its exact administration, membership standards, relationship to regional law,
and any internal credentials remain open.
