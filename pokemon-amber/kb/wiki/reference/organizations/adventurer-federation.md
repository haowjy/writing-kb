# Adventurer Federation

**Status:** drafting

The adventurer federation is a newer international coordinating body built on
top of much older adventurer culture, private guilds, and Pokemon Center
request networks. **Adventurer Federation** and **International Adventurers
Guild** are working descriptions; its exact formal name remains open.

## Historical Foundation

For generations, settlements posted scattered requests for items, Pokemon,
rescues, exploration, salvage, and dangerous wild or Mystery Dungeon work.
Rough local groups were informally called adventurer guilds. They included
explorers, guides, mercenaries, salvagers, rescue crews, treasure hunters, and
bad actors rather than one regulated profession.

That older culture persists. Independent adventurers and private guilds remain
possible, and **adventurer** remains a cultural identity rather than a legal
trainer class.

## International Coordination

About ten years before the story, worsening Mystery Dungeon incidents pushed
reputable guilds, the [Joy and Pokemon Center network](joy-network.md),
[Rangers](ranger-union.md), and governments to establish a more international
body. It coordinates:

- Dungeon requests, warnings, maps, and field reports;
- expeditions and cross-border logistics;
- rescue support and emergency equipment;
- information exchange among guilds, Centers, Rangers, and governments;
- efforts to contain Dungeon outbreaks and keep their effects from spreading
  into inhabited terrain.

Pokemon Centers often serve as local access and logistics nodes. Existing
[quest boards](../world/systems/trainers/quest-system.md) therefore remain part
of the institution's historical base and operating infrastructure rather than
being replaced by a second public job system.

## Status and Limits

- The federation does not replace ordinary trainer licensing.
- The current framework establishes no universal legal Adventurer license or
  rank.
- Local guild membership, professional reputation, expedition requirements,
  and regional permits may matter without redefining adventurer identity.
- Independent crews, private guilds, and disreputable adventurers remain
  outside or alongside the federation.

## Open Questions

- Exact formal name.
- Exact administration, membership standards, and relationship to regional
  law.
- Whether it issues any internal credentials, distinct from a universal legal
  Adventurer license.
