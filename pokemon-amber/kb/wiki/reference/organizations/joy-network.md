# Joy Network

**Status:** drafting

The Joy network is an ancient transregional healing and service tradition that
predates modern international institutions. It remains neutral in mission:
care travels where it is needed rather than serving one region's political or
military authority.

## History

Joys began as nomadic Pokemon healers moving among isolated settlements. They
healed and protected Pokemon, responded to disasters, taught communities how
to care for Pokemon, and maintained neutral sanctuaries or resting places for
travelers and their partners.

As settlements and routes stabilized, Joy healers settled at those service
sites. The sanctuaries developed into clinics and eventually Pokemon Centers,
while the older transregional family and service network endured behind the
local institutions.

## Modern Role

- operate or staff a transregional network of Pokemon care sites;
- preserve neutral healing and disaster-response duties;
- circulate practical care knowledge across regional boundaries;
- provide trusted infrastructure through which requests, warnings, rescue
  support, and expedition logistics can move.

Pokemon Centers' role in the [quest-board system](../world/systems/trainers/quest-system.md)
and the newer [adventurer federation](adventurer-federation.md) extends this
history. It does not make the Joy network subordinate to either institution.

## Jenny Association

Jenny protectors historically accompanied or defended Joy healers, caravans,
sanctuaries, and service routes. Whether the Jennys began as a branch of the
Joy clan or as a distinct allied protector clan is deliberately unresolved;
see the [Jenny network](jenny-network.md).
