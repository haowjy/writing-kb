# Joy Network

**Status:** drafting

The Joy network is an ancient transregional healing tradition. Joys began as
nomadic Pokemon healers serving isolated settlements, travelers, and disaster
zones; their sanctuaries later developed into clinics and Pokemon Centers.

The network still carries care knowledge, disaster aid, and a neutral service
mission across regional boundaries. Pokemon Centers also host [quest
boards](../world/systems/trainers/quest-system.md) and support adventurer
logistics without making the Joy network subordinate to either system.

Jenny protectors historically served alongside Joy healers and sanctuaries;
their exact original relationship remains open. See the [Jenny
network](jenny-network.md).
