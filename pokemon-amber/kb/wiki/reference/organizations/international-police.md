# International Police

**Status:** drafting

The International Police is a recent treaty body for crimes and incidents that
cross regional jurisdictions. The pre-existing [Jenny network](jenny-network.md)
was central to its formation and supplies much of its local institutional
backbone.

## Mandate

The International Police coordinates investigation of:

- criminals who cross regional borders;
- Pokemon and artifact trafficking;
- stolen research and dangerous technology;
- international criminal organizations;
- multi-jurisdictional unexplained, Mystery Dungeon, and legendary incidents.

Local Jenny and other police branches retain ordinary local law enforcement.
The International Police becomes relevant when the crime, evidence, suspects,
or phenomenon crosses jurisdictions or requires a joint investigation.

## Authority

Its authority comes from treaties and host-region cooperation. It is not an
all-powerful global police force with automatic jurisdiction everywhere.
Regional governments can define access, arrest powers, evidence handling, and
the role of local officers within a joint operation.

## Operational Boundaries

- [Adventurers](../world/systems/trainers/adventurer-system.md) explore and
  perform contracted fieldwork.
- International Police investigators pursue crimes, suspects, evidence, and
  criminal organizations.
- [Rangers](ranger-union.md) manage ecosystems, route closures, wild Pokemon,
  rescue, and public safety.

The same incident can require all three, but their mandates remain distinct.
