# International Police

**Status:** drafting

The International Police is a recent treaty body for crimes whose suspects,
evidence, organizations, or effects cross regional jurisdictions. It is
established by the four-region treaty negotiated in Sinnoh roughly 10–12 days
after the Pallet Town attack. The older [Jenny network](jenny-network.md) helps
found it and supplies much of its initial local backbone.

The treaty takes effect at the end of the negotiations, but the new body still
depends on borrowed personnel, temporary offices, and promised funding while
its permanent machinery is built.

Its authority depends on treaties and host-region cooperation; local police
retain ordinary jurisdiction. It coordinates cross-border investigations and
joint operations rather than replacing local police, [Rangers](ranger-union.md),
or adventurers.
