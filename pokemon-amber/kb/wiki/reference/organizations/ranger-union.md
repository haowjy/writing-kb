# Ranger Union

**Status:** drafting

Rangers are wilderness first responders and route wardens, closer to
firefighters and paramedics than police. Their primary mandate is keeping travel
between settlements safe.

## Work and Authority

Rangers:

- patrol routes, post closures and warnings, and clear hazards;
- conduct wilderness search and rescue and disaster response;
- manage dangerous wild Pokemon, habitats, protected zones, and poaching risk;
- maintain stations, communications, and emergency equipment;
- post overflow work to Pokemon Center [quest
  boards](../world/systems/trainers/quest-system.md).

They may inspect licenses, enforce wilderness rules, and command public-safety
responses. On maintained routes and at known Dungeon access points, an ordinary
inspection can include Trainer ID, the number of Pokemon a person carries or
actively controls, and required safety gear. Police own arrests and criminal
investigations; the [International Police](international-police.md) coordinates
cross-border cases; adventurers explore and perform contracted fieldwork; Ace
Trainers serve military and championship roles.

## Proportionate Enforcement

Rangers function as route wardens as well as emergency responders. They issue
warnings, scoldings, civil fines, closures, or a request to leave when the
circumstances warrant; minor child and paperwork violations are not a pretext
for a brutal response. Routine enforcement belongs in the travel world
occasionally, not at every stop.


## Organization

| Level | Function |
|---|---|
| Recruit | Supervised field trainee. |
| Ranger | Full field authority on assigned routes and missions. |
| Senior Ranger | Leads rescue teams and incidents; certifies contractors. |
| Regional command | Oversees stations and interregional crisis coordination. |

Rangers are a government-funded service. Applicants generally need substantial
badge progress, field competence, and medical and wilderness training; exact
regional standards may vary.

## History and Adaptation

The continuity retains the Ranger Union, nature-protection mission, and elite
wilderness specialization from the Pokemon Ranger series, but removes Capture
Stylers: Rangers use standard Pokeballs and permanent teams. Kanto-Johto field
services are integrated with League, Center, and government systems.

Ranger work formalized after Pokeballs enabled professional wilderness teams.
The Kanto secession war later expanded habitat protection, protected-zone and
poaching authority, and early warning for ecosystem disruption. Mt. Silver's
exact role and public account remain open.

Chapter 7 establishes Ranger Requests on the Celadon job board, including Route
7 Spearow flock management, a Mt. Moon wild Pokemon census, and an urgent lost
trainer rescue.
