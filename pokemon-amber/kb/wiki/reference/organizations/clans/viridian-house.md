# Viridian House

**Status:** drafting

Viridian House is a Kanto-only Ground-type breeding lineage centered on Viridian City. It abandoned traditional clan rites generations ago in favor of modern breeding, commercial influence, and control of [Viridian Gym](../../places/kanto/viridian-gym.md). Through Madame Boss's strategic marriage into the house, its legitimate influence became entangled with [Team Rocket](../team-rocket.md).

## Profile

- **Pokemon affinity:** Ground-types, including Nidoking and Rhydon lines.
- **Political position:** a wealthy Kanto family with civic influence through the Gym, not a Johto-style ritual clan.
- **Method:** systematic breeding and training programs rather than inherited rites.

The House's modernization is not evidence that its authority is neutral or purely meritocratic. It is one way Kanto converted older lineage power into commercial and institutional power.

## Giovanni's Inheritance

Giovanni inherits his Gym position and Ground-type lineage from his father's Viridian House connection, while his mother brings Rocket's criminal empire and Silvertail logistics. His father is deliberately unidentified. The marriage gives Rocket access to a respectable Kanto family name and gives the House access to illicit capital and networks.

Giovanni's later Viridian modernization expands this inherited civic base. The public projects provide genuine benefits while also making the House, the Gym, and Giovanni's patronage central to local life. See [Giovanni](../../characters/humans/giovanni.md) and [Viridian Gym](../../places/kanto/viridian-gym.md).

## Political Alignment

House figures and associates can include Kanto nationalists, hardline donors, careerists, and people attracted by Giovanni's practical success. Do not assume every participant in its civic network knows the Rocket connection or shares its crimes.
