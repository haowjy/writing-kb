# Project X

**Status:** drafting

Project X is a secret international compact for strengthening the military and
combat forces of participating rulers and elites. Members selectively share
combat training, aura methods, sacred or artifact knowledge, rare-Pokemon
information, and exceptional-evolution research that their institutions once
hoarded. The benefits are not intended for public distribution.

Participants may include rulers, powerful clans, Champions, noble or royal
families, senior League officials, sacred-site custodians, and trusted scholarly
or technical families. This is a class of participants, not a settled roster.
Project X overlaps with the [old noble bloc](../world/society/clan-system.md)
but neither contains nor represents it.

Mega Evolution is Project X's best current lead. Project X is broader than Mega
Evolution and distinct from [Fuji's](../characters/humans/dr-fuji.md) unstable
forced-evolution serum.

[Lance](../characters/humans/lance.md) is involved on behalf of Indigo,
Blackthorn, or both and has invited [Oak](../characters/humans/professor-oak.md)
to participate. The compact's founder, roster, timing, administration, Lance's
role, and the invitation's timing and terms remain open in [Project X
planning](../../planning/project-x.md).
