# Project X

**Status:** drafting

Project X is a secret joint international compact for strengthening the
military and combat forces of its participating rulers and elites. Its members
pool combat training, aura methods, sacred or artifact knowledge, rare-Pokemon
information, and exceptional-evolution research that their institutions once
hoarded. The participating members share access to that work; the benefits are
not intended for public distribution or for people outside the compact.

Participants may include rulers, powerful clans, Champions, noble or royal
families, senior League officials, sacred-site custodians, and trusted scholarly
or technical families. This is a class of participants, not a settled roster.
Project X overlaps with the [old noble bloc](../world/society/clan-system.md)
but neither contains nor represents it.

Mega Evolution is Project X's central joint investigation and best current
lead. Project X is broader than Mega Evolution and distinct from
[Fuji's](../characters/humans/dr-fuji.md) unstable forced-evolution serum.

[Lance](../characters/humans/lance.md) is involved on behalf of Indigo,
Blackthorn, or both. [Oak](../characters/humans/professor-oak.md) was never
invited: Lance and the Blackthorn leadership consider him dangerous and do not
trust him with the compact. Oak learned that some kind of secret project exists
through an undisclosed source, but does not know its methods or full membership.
The compact's founder, roster, timing, administration, Lance's role, and the
source of Oak's information remain open in [Project X planning](../../planning/project-x.md).
