# Project X

**Status:** drafting

Project X is a secret joint international compact for strengthening the
military and combat forces of its participating rulers and elites. Its members
pool combat training, aura methods, sacred or artifact knowledge, rare-Pokemon
information, and exceptional-evolution research that their institutions once
hoarded. The participating members share access to that work; the benefits are
not intended for public distribution or for people outside the compact.

Participants may include rulers, powerful clans, Champions, noble or royal
families, senior League officials, sacred-site custodians, and trusted scholarly
or technical families. This is a class of participants, not a complete roster.
Project X overlaps with the [old noble bloc](../world/society/clan-system.md)
but neither contains nor represents it. Professor Rowan participates because
he is the compact's relevant expert on Pokemon evolution.

Mega Evolution is Project X's central joint investigation and best current
lead. The work increasingly indicates that the bond between a Pokemon and its
trainer may be causal rather than incidental. Project X is broader than Mega
Evolution, and Mega Evolution remains distinct from [Fuji's](../characters/humans/dr-fuji.md)
fatal, unstable forced-evolution serum.

## Oak's exclusion and Rowan's consultation

[Oak](../characters/humans/professor-oak.md) remains formally excluded. [Lance](../characters/humans/lance.md),
Blackthorn, and other members consider him politically and militarily dangerous
because of his wartime record, influence, regional-security implications, and
the threat he poses to elite control of Project X's knowledge. Rowan's opposing
view is scientific: excluding the leading expert on Pokemon bonds deprives the
Mega Evolution investigation of the expertise its evidence requires.

Rowan and Oak are longstanding, publicly known academic colleagues and friends.
Rowan is the older, more senior scholar, so their regular correspondence is
unremarkable. Rowan has let Oak learn that Project X exists and now consults him
privately as the bond evidence grows stronger. Oak studies the bond side of the
problem while Rowan studies the evolution side; Rowan can quietly carry Oak's
ideas back into Project X without making Oak a member.

The consultation remains compartmentalized. Rowan shares sanitized observations
and questions, not the compact's full roster, sites, artifacts, military plans,
or unrestricted archive. Lance knows Oak has somehow learned something about
the compact, but he does not know Rowan was the source or that Rowan now
consults Oak. Rowan is an obvious possible suspect among many professors and
former Oak contacts, but ordinary academic correspondence is not proof.

The compact's founder, remaining roster, timing, administration, Lance's role,
the later extent of Rowan's disclosures, and whether Rowan is ever exposed
remain open in [Project X planning](../../planning/project-x.md).
