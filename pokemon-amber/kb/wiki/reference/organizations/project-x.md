# Project X

**Status:** drafting

Project X is a secret international compact for strengthening the military and
combat forces of participating rulers and elites. It is international
cooperation without public distribution: members share exceptional knowledge
they historically hoarded, but only inside a circle whose definition of
"trusted" is rooted in lineage, office, inherited duty, and reputation.

## Participants and Purpose

The compact draws selectively from world rulers, powerful clans, Champions,
noble or royal families, senior League officials, sacred-site custodians, and
a few trusted scholarly or technical families. This describes the kinds of
power represented, not a settled membership roster.

Project X overlaps with the world's [old noble clan
bloc](../world/society/clan-system.md) but does not contain or represent it.
Ancient houses outside the compact remain a broader political class with their
own interests, and participating houses need not reduce all of their politics
to Project X.

Its members share knowledge that can strengthen their own forces:

- combat techniques and clan training;
- aura methods;
- legends and sacred-site knowledge;
- information about rare artifacts and Pokemon;
- exceptional evolution and transformation research.

The restriction is structural and ideological, not a temporary security
measure on a future public-benefit program. Project X does not intend to spread
the resulting power broadly.

## Mystery Dungeons and Public Justification

Participants may cite worsening [Mystery Dungeon](../world/phenomena/mystery-dungeons.md)
devastation, wild attacks, or other global dangers as justification. The
settled core purpose is nevertheless elite military strengthening, not Dungeon
relief or international public safety.

## Mega Evolution

Mega Evolution is Project X's most promising current lead: dramatic temporary
power unlocked through trainer-Pokemon coordination. It is distinct from
[Fuji's](../characters/humans/dr-fuji.md) unstable forced-evolution serum.

Project X is the umbrella for exceptional combat and evolution knowledge, not
a synonym for Mega Evolution alone.

## Lance and Oak

[Lance](../characters/humans/lance.md) is involved and represents Indigo or
Blackthorn interests within the compact. He has previously invited
[Oak](../characters/humans/professor-oak.md) to participate. Lance's exact role
and the timing of the invitation remain open.

Exact founding, ownership, administration, and membership are not settled; see
[Project X planning questions](../../planning/project-x.md).
