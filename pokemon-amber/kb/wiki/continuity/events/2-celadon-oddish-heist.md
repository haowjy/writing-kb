# Event: Celadon Oddish Heist

## Overview
A sickly girl (Kaede) steals a Celadon Gym Oddish amidst a misunderstanding, seeking to replicate HP UP to treat her brother's illness.

## Timeline of Events
- Amber trespasses greenhouse; later suspected of being an accomplice. ([Ch.5](https://github.com/haowjy/pokemon-amber/blob/main/story/ch5/chapter5.md), [Ch.7](https://github.com/haowjy/pokemon-amber/blob/main/story/ch7/chapter7.md))
- Security footage reveals the thief but not her face; Amber recognizes her from their earlier collision and becomes the only available eyewitness. Erika joins the investigation. ([Ch.8](https://github.com/haowjy/pokemon-amber/blob/main/story/ch8/chapter8.md))
- Department Store inquiry gathers leads. Mary requires Amber to hold her hand through the Game Corner and the recognition because Amber alone can identify the girl. When the girl flees, Mary lets go and says "Stay here"; Amber follows with Ditto's occupied Pokeball but does not release it. Mary's Bellsprout captures the girl, who pleads for her brother's life and remains unnamed. ([Ch.9](https://github.com/haowjy/pokemon-amber/blob/main/story/ch9/chapter9.md))
- The girl's identity as Kaede and motive are revealed; Erika treats Hiro and authorizes Oddish to remain temporarily. ([Ch.10](https://github.com/haowjy/pokemon-amber/blob/main/story/ch10/chapter10.md))

## Aftermath
- Mary's suspension context; improved standing through resolution. Amber departs Celadon arc shortly after. ([Ch.9](https://github.com/haowjy/pokemon-amber/blob/main/story/ch9/chapter9.md), [Ch.10](https://github.com/haowjy/pokemon-amber/blob/main/story/ch10/chapter10.md))
