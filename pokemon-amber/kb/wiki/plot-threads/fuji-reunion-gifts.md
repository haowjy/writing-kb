# Fuji's Reunion Gifts

**Continuity spine for the flowers and the alcohol, Ch 6 through Ch 14**

Fuji stages the same reunion twice. Each attempt is flowers plus wine. The first
fails and he drinks the gift; the second ends with him blowing Delia's door in.

He got sober when Giovanni recruited him and stayed dry about twelve years. Ch 6
breaks that. Every chapter after it is further down the same slide, and no
character ever remarks on it.

## The Diagnosis Between Attempts

Ch 13's *"Do things right again"* points back at Ch 11. He concludes the premise
was sound and the execution was sloppy, so the retry corrects the flower, the
clothes, and the wine. Roses in Ch 11 exist to make that self-diagnosis legible
— if he had brought dahlias the first time, he would have nothing left to fix.

The dahlia is his own hybrid, named after her, given at the proposal. Bringing it
in Ch 14 is not a nice gesture repeated; it is him reasserting a claim.

## Ch 13 and Ch 14 Are the Same Day

They run in parallel from opposite POVs. Ch 13 is Fuji's Day 2; Ch 14 is Amber's
Day 2. Stephen makes pancakes in Ch 14's morning, leaves, and dies in Ch 13's
late afternoon — which is why Delia spends Ch 14's dinner checking the clock, and
why Fuji arrives at that door the same night.

Anything inserted into either chapter has to hold against the other.

## Drafting Rules

- Never name it. No character comments on his drinking; the prose never calls him
  drunk. Impairment shows only in what he does.
- Never measure it. No weights, durations, or counts anywhere near the bottle.
  Three such lines were cut from Ch 11 during revision (*heavy enough that he had
  to brace it against his chest*, *He was in there a long time*, *He wasn't in
  there two minutes*) because measurement is the camera lingering, and the thread
  depends on it never lingering.
- Call the wine *wine*, so a reader who remembers Ch 11 can connect the bottle in
  Ch 13.
- No relapse language. He never thinks about being sober, quitting, or how long
  it has been. The twelve years are structure for the writer only.
- Fuji is never careless. He is deliberate and wrong. He reproduces the form of
  the thing — roses are the idea of romance, the clone is the idea of a daughter.

## What Does Not Need Writing

The Ch 11 → Ch 13 break covers a night and a morning, and it needs nothing. The
bottle being most of the way down by afternoon reports the drinking; the roses
being absent and dahlias being on the shopping list reports what happened to the
flowers. Writing either one costs the thread more than it pays.

The second bottle is the same. Ch 13 plans *"maybe some of the best wine"* and Ch
14 contains no alcohol. *Maybe* is doing the work. Whether he bought it is not a
question the chapter raises.

## Open

- **Does Delia still grow dahlias?** The only unresolved item that changes a
  published line. Costs one clause in Ch 12 and decides how Ch 14 reads: either she
  kept the flower he weaponized, or he stands at her door naming the favorite of a
  woman who stopped existing fifteen years ago.
