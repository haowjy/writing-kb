# Event: Forest Path Incident

## Overview
Dr. Fuji ambushes Stephen Ketchum on a forest path near Pallet Town and, using Ditto (Gyarados-form) and Magmar, kills Stephen and his Poliwag; Magmar incinerates the bodies.

## Timeline of Events
- Fuji lies in wait with surveillance photos and escalating obsession. He has spent the afternoon on a fallen log defacing and tearing the photos, drinking the bottle of wine he bought for the Pallet Town reunion that failed. ([Ch.13](../../story/ch13/chapter13.md))
- Orders Ditto-Gyarados: Dragon Rage; orders Hyper Beam; Magmar finishes; bodies burned. ([Ch.13](../../story/ch13/chapter13.md))

## Aftermath
- Fuji plans "reunion" with Delia; heads to acquire flowers, wine, better clothes. This is his *second* staging of the same reunion --- he already tried it with a gas-station bouquet and a bottle in Ch 11 and was thrown off the property. ([Ch.13](../../story/ch13/chapter13.md))

## Notes
Fuji is not a habitual drinker at this point in the story. He was sober for roughly twelve years and relapsed in Ch 6 after seeing surveillance photos of Delia with Stephen. The wine on the log is early in that slide, and he is impaired rather than drunk --- not enough to be an excuse, enough to matter. See [Dr. Fuji --- The Alcohol Timeline][fuji-alcohol].
