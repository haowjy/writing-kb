# Cut the Old-World Opening; Make the Backpack Mysterious

**Scope**: Ch 1 primarily, Ch 3 L143, and every later line that treats Amber's
previous life as established fact
**Severity**: Structural --- changes what the reader is allowed to know
**Status**: Queued. Do not start until the Fuji alcohol thread is closed.

---

## What the Author Wants

Two halves of one change.

1. **Remove the isekai death scene.** Ch 1 currently opens in the old world: a
   Nuzlocke run on a phone, a Discord raid alert, a run into traffic, a truck on
   University Avenue. The author's read is that it plays as cringe.
2. **Make the backpack's story much more mysterious.** Ch 3 L143 hands the reader
   its provenance in a dash clause:

   > The ancient backpack---a relic of Amber's past life---sat awkwardly on my
   > shoulders, its straps adjusted as tight as they would go.

   It is the dead Amber's bag, kept twelve years, given to her clone. The prose
   states that instead of letting the reader arrive at it.

These are the same problem. The opening establishes where she came from, so
everything downstream can reference it flatly. Cut the opening and objects like
the backpack have to carry it, which is what makes them eerie.

---

## Why It Is Not Just a Deletion

Ch 1 currently does four jobs in the old-world section, and only the first is
being cut:

| Job | Survives the cut? |
|---|---|
| Establishes she died in a mundane world | No --- that is the point |
| Establishes she is an adult in a child's body | Must move; it drives everything |
| Establishes she knows Pokemon as a *game* | Must move; drives every metagame beat |
| Plants the Shadow Mewtwo raid alert against the real Mewtwo she wakes to | Decide deliberately |

That fourth one is load-bearing in a way that is easy to miss: the raid alert on
her phone and the actual Mewtwo tearing the lab open are the same joke, and the
irony only exists if the reader saw the phone. Removing it costs that.

Downstream dependencies to audit before touching anything:

- Ch 3 L143 backpack (`a relic of Amber's past life`)
- Ch 3 L41 (`lazy weekend afternoons from another life`)
- Ch 5, Ch 7-9 --- her game knowledge used as fact
- Ch 11's `First Ditto, and now him.` --- already cut the old-life clause once
- Ch 12 L65, the name question: *was it the one from college? From my previous
  life?*
- Ch 12 L145 (`A couple weeks ago, in my dorm room`)

## Constraint Already Established

Amber's old life is **memory only**. She has no access to anything that happened
on the other side after she died. Any line that narrates the other side as fact is
invention dressed as fact. Cutting the opening makes this constraint stronger, not
weaker --- afterward the reader cannot verify any of her memories either.

## Open

  it from her voice and her competence?
- If the raid-alert irony goes, does anything replace it, or does Ch 1 simply open
  in the tank?
- Whose backpack does the reader think it is, and when do they work it out?
