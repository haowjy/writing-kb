# Professor Oak: Dialogue and Scene Presence

Load this file when Oak appears. Ch. 16 uses a distinct panoramic register,
not Oak close third; it may range beyond one character's immediate access while
still keeping Oak's choices legible.

## Core behavior

Oak's expertise appears through priorities: protect people, read Pokemon
condition and capability, limit collateral damage, then press the opponent for
useful information. He need not narrate a procedure or solve every problem
instantly for the reader to see that expertise.

## Scene presence

### Triage before discussion

> "Professor Samuel Oak did not waste any time on words." [ch16]

The following action should earn the line. In [ch16], Oak removes children from
immediate danger before confronting Fuji.

### Public radius

> "Oak glanced at the crowd that had formed across the street, and the additional policemen that had pulled up." [ch16]

The glance changes the encounter's constraints: bystanders and police belong in
the tactical picture.

### Pokemon as partners

> "Arcanine, Extreme Speed! Tank it if you must!" [ch16]

The command coordinates capability around protection, accepting cost rather
than treating Pokemon as disposable tools.

## Dialogue by audience

With Fuji, Oak can stay neutral enough to keep him talking. With children, he
reassures before offering the amount of truth they can use. With Amber
privately, he is careful about facts he cannot verify; gentleness changes his
delivery while curiosity shapes his questions.
