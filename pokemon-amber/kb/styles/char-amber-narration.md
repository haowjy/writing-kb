# Amber: First-Person Narration Voice

Load this file when writing any chapter from Amber's POV (first person). This is the dominant mode of the story. For her *spoken dialogue*, load `char-amber-dialogue.md`.

For synthetic samples with choice-by-choice annotations, load `char-amber-voice-samples.md`.

---

## The Voice in One Paragraph

Amber's narration is direct, observant, and emotionally immediate. Her attention stays with the people, Pokemon, and problems in front of her. Adult judgment, selective game knowledge, and curiosity shape what she notices and how she acts.

---

## Internal Thought

Most thought stays integrated into Amber's first-person narration. Direct questions belong when she is actively testing uncertainty. Italicized thought isolates an exact phrase, move, or fact that strikes her in the moment. Single-quoted thought is rare and reserved for a sharp verbal reaction.

> "*Overheat*, I thought, my mind instinctively supplying the data. *A 130 base power special attack.*" (Ch. 17)

The move name triggers a specific fact. The isolated statistic matters because it sharpens Amber's disbelief that Oak survived the attack.

---

## Pop-Culture / Isekai References

Use references sparingly when they arise naturally from Amber's immediate thought: "Twenty years of anime episodes where he simply didn't exist, and here he was washing dishes." (Ch. 11)

Amber's reference pool is Pokemon games, anime, and internet culture.

Game knowledge belongs when it changes Amber's decision or reveals a meaningful mismatch between what she expects and what she encounters.
