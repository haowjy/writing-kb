# Dr. Fuji: POV Voice and Dialogue

Load this file for Fuji's close-third chapters (Chs. 6 and 13) or whenever he
speaks.

## Core behavior

Fuji tries to correct reality until it matches the family, status, or
scientific success he believes he deserves. Family language, professional
language, and commands can occupy the same exchange. The register he chooses
shows what he is trying to control.

## Close-third narration

### Ritual before diagnosis

Fuji's meals, clothes, flowers, and drinks make his attempted reunion concrete.
Let the object handling and sequence carry the disturbance before adding a
narratorial verdict.

> "Three plates, three sets of chopsticks, three glasses of juice, all arranged on a table meant for one." [ch6]

### Correction and rationalization

Fuji notices differences as defects in his reconstruction. His self-
justification may enter close third as if it were reasonable; the surrounding
behavior lets readers judge it. Do not add a second narrator voice to certify
that he is distorted.

> "He watched her pick at her food, noted how she held her chopsticks---all wrong, nothing like before---and added it to his mental list of differences to fix later." [ch6]

### Escalation

When control holds, Fuji's sentences tend toward formal completion. When it
fails, repetition, fragments, and commands can take over at the point his plan
stops producing the expected response.

## Spoken dialogue

- With Amber, Fuji uses family roles and corrective authority.
- With peers and rivals, he reaches for credentials, hierarchy, and technical
  superiority.
- With Pokemon, he frames creation as ownership and obedience.
- With violence, procedural language can sit beside extreme harm.
