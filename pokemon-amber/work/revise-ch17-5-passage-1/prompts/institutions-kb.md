SETTLED CURRENT DIRECTION

1. International development is layered, not controlled by one organization. Old transregional family/service networks coexist with recent treaty institutions, voluntary/professional adventurer structures, and a secret elite military compact.

2. Joy network/history:
- Joys historically began as nomadic Pokemon healers traveling between isolated settlements, healing/protecting Pokemon, responding to disasters, teaching care, and maintaining neutral sanctuaries/resting places.
- As settlements/routes stabilized, they settled and those sites developed into clinics and Pokemon Centers.
- Their transregional network predates modern international institutions and retains a neutral/service mission.

3. Jenny history/status:
- Jennys historically served as protectors associated with Joy healers/sanctuaries/caravans and later expanded into general peacekeeping and local policing as cities formed.
- Jenny branches recently helped found the International Police and now form much of the local institutional backbone.
- UNRESOLVED: whether Jennys were literally once a branch of the Joy clan or were always a distinct allied protector clan. Record both as an open decision; do not canonize either.
- Jennys are decentralized regional branches, not necessarily one perfectly unified sovereign organization.

4. Adventurer culture/institutions:
- Historically there were scattered requests for items, Pokemon, rescues, exploration, and wild/Dungeon work. Rough local groups were informally called adventurer guilds. They included explorers, guides, mercenaries, salvagers, rescue crews, treasure hunters, and bad actors.
- Adventurer remains a cultural identity; independent adventurers and private guilds remain possible.
- Around the past ~10 years, worsening/increasing Mystery Dungeon incidents led reputable guilds, Joy/Pokemon Center networks, Rangers, and governments to establish a more international Adventurers Guild/federation.
- This newer body coordinates Dungeon requests, maps, reports, expeditions, rescue support, equipment, warnings, and cross-border logistics, often through Pokemon Centers.
- It does NOT replace ordinary trainer licensing or necessarily create a universal legal Adventurer license/rank. Preserve the distinction between trainer license and adventurer identity.
- Existing Pokemon Center quest boards remain valid and become part of this history/infrastructure rather than being discarded.

5. Mystery Dungeon operational framing:
- Mystery Dungeons remain ancient natural phenomena, not portals; Ultra Wormholes remain distinct; human activity destabilizes them but does not create them.
- The newer Guild is intended to push back, contain, or suppress worsening Dungeons and keep their effects from spreading into inhabited terrain.
- Fantasy dungeon-break influence is being considered: unstable Dungeons may overflow/break, with distorted terrain, weather/habitat effects, displaced/aggressive Pokemon, looping geography, and danger spreading beyond known boundaries.
- Expeditions may colloquially claim to "defeat" Dungeons, but in practice they contain outbreaks, rescue victims, remove aggravating factors, and force effects back. Whether any Dungeon can truly be permanently destroyed is unknown.
- UNRESOLVED cosmology: literal physical expansion/new land versus destabilization of existing Dungeons plus discovery of new sites creating the appearance of spread. Mark open and avoid hard canon beyond outbreak/containment direction.

6. International Police:
- Recently created treaty body, with the established Jenny network central to its formation.
- Handles cross-border crime and international phenomena: criminals crossing regions, Pokemon/artifact trafficking, stolen research, dangerous technology, international criminal organizations, and multi-jurisdictional unexplained/Dungeon/legendary incidents.
- Local Jenny/police branches keep local law-enforcement roles.
- International Police authority depends on treaties and host-region cooperation; it is not an all-powerful global police force with automatic authority everywhere.
- Distinguish mandates: adventurers explore/perform contracted fieldwork; International Police investigate crimes/pursue suspects; Rangers handle ecosystems, route closures, wild Pokemon, and public safety.

7. Project X:
- A secret international compact/cooperative project established by selected world rulers, powerful clans, Champions, noble/royal families, senior League officials, sacred-site custodians, and a few trusted scholars/technical families.
- Its true purpose is to bolster the participating rulers' own military/combat forces by sharing knowledge they historically hoarded: combat techniques, aura methods, clan training, legends, rare artifacts/Pokemon information, and exceptional evolution/transformation research.
- The restriction to elites is structural and ideological: "trusted" is defined through lineage, office, inherited duty, and reputation. It is not a public-benefit institution and does not intend to distribute resulting power broadly.
- Project X may invoke increasing Mystery Dungeon devastation/wild attacks/global danger as justification, but its current committed core is an elite military-strengthening agreement.
- Mega Evolution is currently its most promising lead: dramatic temporary power unlocked through trainer-Pokemon coordination, distinct from Fuji's unstable forced-evolution serum.
- Project X is best treated as an umbrella for exceptional combat/evolution knowledge rather than simply synonymous with Mega Evolution.
- Lance is involved/represents Indigo or Blackthorn and has previously invited Oak. UNRESOLVED: exact founding date, whether Lance personally started it, whether it predates his Championship, exact administration, membership list, and whether Blackthorn leads it. Do not canonize a roster.
- Potential members discussed but NOT settled: Blackthorn Dragon Clan, Ecruteak houses, Kalos government/Tower traditions, an invented Pryce/Mahogany ice clan, Diamond/Pearl successor traditions, Celestic custodians, Stone family, Opelucid lineage.

8. Political/thematic distinction:
- Joy network distributes healing wherever needed.
- International Adventurers Guild/federation pools field knowledge and operations.
- International Police crosses borders to prevent them sheltering criminals.
- Project X embraces international cooperation while hoarding its benefits among rulers/elites.

9. Rough chronology direction (planning-grade, not exact canon dates unless existing docs support them):
- Centuries/long ago: nomadic Joy healers and associated Jenny protectors.
- As cities/routes develop: sanctuaries become Centers; Jenny branches become local police.
- For generations: rough local adventurer guilds/request networks.
- About 10 years before story: international adventurer federation develops in response to Dungeon activity.
- Within recent years/decade: Jenny networks help establish International Police.
- Project X is recent, but exact timing/open ownership remain unresolved.
