# Redraft Chapter 17.5, Passage 1

Write a new opening passage for Chapter 17.5. This replaces the rejected immediate-aftermath/guard-negotiation draft. Draft only Passage 1 and save it to `story/ch17.5/passage1-draft.md`. Do not edit any other file.

## Settled new direction

- The scene occurs several days after the Pallet attack, not the same morning.
- Dr. Fuji is in a secure detention/medical wing.
- After a very short Oak cold open, Giovanni and Blaine already have access.
  Begin the main scene with them approaching Fuji's cell.
- Do not show or explain conversations with guards, wardens, police, or officials.
- Blaine accompanies Giovanni because the Magmar that died after Fuji injected it was originally Blaine's partner, former partner, or a Pokemon Blaine entrusted to Fuji. The exact legal ownership wording need not be explained in this passage.
- Persian accompanies Giovanni and should read as his constant partner rather
  than a special weapon brought out for this visit.
- Blaine carries another Magmar but does not release it during Passage 1. He
  will release Magmar in the middle of the later interrogation, after Fuji's
  treatment of the dead Magmar becomes intolerable.
- Remove Madame Boss and the explosion from this version of the interlude.
- The eventual order will sound like an execution: `We'll have Dr. Fuji killed.` Author truth is that Fuji's public identity will be killed and he will be moved to a Rocket black site. Do not reach that order in Passage 1.
- Whether Blaine knows about the staged death remains unresolved. Do not disclose it.

## Passage function

Reorient readers after a long publication gap through the consequences of the Pallet attack:

- Magmar died after Fuji's forced transformation experiment.
- Fuji was captured and injured.
- Oak survived but was badly hurt.
- Fuji is now isolated and awaiting disposition.

Do not summarize these facts in a recap paragraph. Let the cold open, corridor,
Blaine's behavior, Persian, and Fuji's condition carry them.

## Reader effect

Controlled dread. Blaine should feel personally furious and grief-stricken
without delivering an explanatory speech. Giovanni should be calmer and more
difficult to read. Persian is composed and familiar with Giovanni. The second
Magmar remains latent in its Pokeball until a later passage.

## POV and prose

- Use an external, camera-close third person rather than deep Giovanni interiority. This allows the later staged-death intent to remain fair-play ambiguous.
- Match Chapters 6, 13, and 16 and the KB tone/tension guidance.
- Selective detail only.
- No generic villain menace, cinematic screenplay shorthand, or explanatory psychology.
- Project punctuation and spelling conventions apply.

## Shape

- Target 500-800 words total.
- Open with a 100-200 word cold open continuing the aftermath of Chapter 16:
  Oak confirms Magmar is dead, notices a broken fragment from its transformed
  cannon-arm and residue in the discarded syringe, and carefully collects or
  preserves samples. Do not name Mega Evolution or explain his conclusion.
- Use a scene break, then move several days forward.
- Begin the main scene with Giovanni, Persian, and Blaine already inside the
  detention corridor.
- Establish their contrasting states while they walk.
- Let Fuji hear or notice them before the door/cell opens if useful.
- End when Giovanni and Blaine enter, Fuji recognizes the dead Magmar's connection, or Giovanni begins the first question.
