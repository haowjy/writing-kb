# Draft Chapter 17.5, Passage 1 only

Write the opening passage of Chapter 17.5 for Pokemon: Ambertwo. This is a fresh-draft prose task, not an outline or critique. Draft only Passage 1, ending when Giovanni has secured private access to Fuji and the cell door closes (or an equally clean threshold immediately before questioning begins). Target roughly 700-1,000 words, but obey the scene rather than padding.

## Reader effect

This chapter returns after a long publication gap and follows Chapter 17's hospital aftermath. Reorient readers through concrete aftermath rather than a recap paragraph. Giovanni should feel more dangerous because he enters openly as a respected Gym Leader and civic figure. The facility staff yield through status, procedure, and quiet pressure, not theatrical villain behavior. Fuji should be present as the disgraced prisoner at the end, but do not begin his backstory or interrogation yet.

Create controlled institutional unease. Leave Madam, Fuji's survival outcome, and the coming attack unresolved.

## POV and style

- Use third-person limited through Giovanni for this interlude.
- Keep his interiority sparse and practical. Do not explain his entire ideology or future plans.
- Match the existing project's grounded, slightly gritty prose.
- Use selective detail. Each object or procedural obstacle must reveal aftermath, authority, or risk.
- Trust the reader. Do not label Giovanni as intimidating, powerful, calculating, or sinister; make staff behavior demonstrate it.
- Avoid generic noir, purple menace, villain quips, and cinematic screenplay shorthand.
- Follow project conventions: ASCII quotes, `---` for em dashes, `...` for ellipses, and established Pokemon spelling.

## Required content

- Establish the immediate aftermath of the Pallet attack and Oak's injury through the facility's condition, staff strain, news presence, paperwork, or conversation. Do not summarize Chapter 17.
- Giovanni arrives by walking through the front entrance. No teleportation.
- Persian accompanies him.
- He uses his legitimate public status as Viridian Gym Leader and an important Kanto figure to gain access.
- The warden or responsible officer initially has a procedural reason to resist private access to Fuji.
- Giovanni overcomes that resistance without revealing Team Rocket or making an overt threat.
- Preserve plausible deniability: a bystander could interpret this as a prominent Gym Leader assisting an urgent investigation.
- End at the threshold of the private conversation with Fuji.
