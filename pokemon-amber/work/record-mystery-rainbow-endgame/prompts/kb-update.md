- Add a fifth major saga thread centered on increasing Mystery Dungeon instability and exploration.

- Ecological damage and intensive human activity increasingly destabilize them: they become more frequent, volatile, expansive, and dangerous, with dungeon-like spatial conditions beginning to intrude into ordinary environments.
- This works broadly like the story world's global-warming analogue: a cumulative systemic crisis rather than a new singular evil Pokemon or mastermind.

### Giovanni, Ultra Space, and Rainbow Rocket
- Giovanni eventually treats other universes as additional territory and replacement worlds rather than accepting ecological or political limits.
- Ultra Wormhole research becomes his route beyond one damaged world and the basis for deliberate conquest of other universes.
- The causal/thematic endpoint is Rainbow Rocket: an interdimensional imperial organization exploiting and conquering multiple Pokemon realities. Expansion exports consequences outward and can perpetuate the same destabilization.
- After Giovanni loses the main-world war, he escapes through an Ultra Wormhole.
- Amber and a small group pursue him through Ultra Space.
- The pursuit encounters many different Pokemon worlds, drawing freely from the anime, manga, games, alternate outcomes, post-apocalyptic settings, villain-victory timelines, Pokemon-only societies, and original speculative worlds.
- This multiverse exploration is the final saga and provides the story with late creative freedom. Ultra Space should open up late so earlier local stakes still matter.
- The final battle of the story is against Rainbow Rocket.
- Giovanni remains the central antagonist; avoid reducing the endgame to an unintegrated cameo/boss rush.
- Exact worlds, party membership, number of worlds, wormhole navigation rules, how long Rainbow Rocket takes to form, whether Giovanni prepared contacts before the war, and exact final objective remain open.
- This adaptation may deliberately differ from USUM: our Giovanni can lose the main-world war and then become/found the Rainbow Rocket Giovanni, rather than necessarily originating in a reality where he already won.
