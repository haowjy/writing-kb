## Hard constraints

- **POV is Fuji, 3rd person limited.** The reader connects trigger to response with no help
  from the narration.
- **No interiority around either the refusal or the reversal.** He does not weigh it,
  justify it, or notice himself doing it. He asks for water. Later he asks for whiskey.
- **Do not signal significance.** No held beat before he answers. No unsteady hands. No
  noticing the bottle earlier in the scene. No line break for emphasis. No narrator nudge.
- **Domino does not react.** No smirk, no knowing look, no pause. She is a junior operative
  who did good work for a senior scientist and wants credit for it. She has no idea what she
  is watching.
- **Whiskey, not wine.** Whiskey is his coping drink for the rest of the story. Wine belongs
  to a separate thread in Ch 11 and Ch 13.
- **Never state or imply he had stopped drinking.** No "first drink in years," no
  hesitation that reads as relapse, nothing.
- Do not slow the scene. The folder stays the focus.
