SETTLED: ANCIENT KANTO AND COLONIAL INTEGRATION
- Until roughly 100-120 years before the story, Kanto was populated by scattered small tribes, villages, and local communities separated by dangerous wilderness and powerful wild Pokemon. It was not literally empty and did have local cultures/traditions, but no unified Kanto government, pan-Kanto political identity, or entrenched pan-regional institutions.
- Johto was substantially more institutionally and technically advanced at the time: established government, writing/records, trainer clans, Pokemon training/Pokeball traditions, roads, commerce, medicine, and organized force. This is pre-industrial institutional advantage; modern Kanto later surpasses Johto in industrial science/manufacturing.
- Johto's incorporation of Kanto was colonial integration, not a wholly peaceful voluntary compact and not necessarily one single conquest war.
- Over generations, Johto-led institutions consolidated disparate eastern peoples into one administered territory called Kanto. The process mixed treaties/co-option, settlement, legal and economic integration, and coercion. Colonial cultural loss, unequal power, and resentment are settled at the structural level; exact atrocities, removal policies, land regime, schooling/language suppression, individual tribes, and campaigns remain open and must not be invented.
- Kanto gradually requested and gained municipal/regional powers and representation from the Johto-dominated order. Its shared identity formed partly through that autonomy struggle.
- Kanto later industrialized, grew wealthy, and surpassed Johto technologically/economically while older Johto institutions retained prestige and influence. Kanto resentment comes from remaining governed within structures created during colonial dependence.
- This materially revises any active claim that Kanto and Johto were equal partners from the beginning or that ancient unification was purely voluntary.
- Preserve moral complexity: Johto institutions produced real infrastructure/integration benefits, but those do not erase colonial coercion. Modern Kanto's independence grievance is substantially legitimate.
- Modern Kanto population composition (descendants of local peoples vs Johto settlers vs mixed, and rural/urban distribution) remains open.
- Exact name/date of the original incorporation government, exact tribal cultures, and exact colonial methods remain open.

SETTLED: GIOVANNI'S LATE DIRECTION
- Giovanni eventually seeks everything important to fall under Kanto rule or Kanto influence: global Kanto hegemony.

It lies in appropriating legitimate grievance for domination, conflating Kanto's interests with his regime/personal control, denying genuine autonomy to others, and expanding “security” until independent power itself is treated as a threat.
