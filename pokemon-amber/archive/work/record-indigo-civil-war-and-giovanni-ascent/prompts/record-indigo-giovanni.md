Settled replacement history:
- Kanto and Johto already belonged to a unified Indigo League before the war.
- Johto had the older institutions, historical clans, hereditary Pokemon lineages, ecological/breeding knowledge, and traditional power.
- Kanto developed the stronger modern industrial, scientific, urban, communications, manufacturing, and technological base.
- Kanto separatists came to view Johto and its clans as leeches on Kanto technology and wealth. This is a nationalist claim, not neutral historical fact; preserve Johto's defensible counterposition.
- Oak, from Kanto, became unified Indigo Champion briefly. His victory restored Kanto pride and became a symbol/mandate for a secession movement he did not originally want.
- Under intense elite, institutional, and public pressure, Oak briefly supported/fought for Kanto secession, believing force could produce a short conflict and negotiated autonomy/independence.
- Oak bears real culpability: he authorized/participated enough that he cannot be reduced to an innocent puppet.
- He quickly became horrified by the bloodshed and began seeking negotiation. Negotiations and demobilization did not necessarily end violence instantly.
- Oak ultimately ended/abandoned the secession effort, resigned or relinquished the Championship, and supported Lance/Blackthorn-associated Johto authority as the reunification successor.
- Exact legal succession procedure, exact war duration, and the precise role/timing of Mt. Silver remain OPEN unless the existing canon and conversation clearly settle them. Do not invent answers.
- Kanto nationalist memory: Oak proved Kanto could lead, began the independence struggle, lost his nerve, surrendered Kanto freedom, and returned the Championship to Johto clan power.
- Johto memory: Oak used unified authority to participate in an illegal Kanto secession.
- Moderate/history view: Oak made a grave pressured choice, recognized it, and prevented worse catastrophe.

Settled Giovanni direction:
- Giovanni replaces Lance through the lawful unified Indigo Elite Four + Champion gauntlet. He becomes unified Indigo Champion, not merely Kanto Champion.
- His decisive advantage comes from years of scientific development and Mewtwo #2, who is raised as a genuine loyal partner rather than controlled as a disposable weapon. Chapter 6's “Then it can understand reason” remains the central seed.
- The battles themselves are real and legitimate under League rules; corrupt officials may enable certification, access, secrecy, or regulatory clearance, but must not falsify the battles or force Lance to lose.
- Giovanni is primarily Napoleon-modeled: organizer, modernizer, personal military/battle prestige, institutional competence, centralization, expansion, and eventual overreach.
- Selected rise mechanics may resemble authoritarian ascent after humiliation/grievance (legal/institutional capture, propaganda, elite underestimation, crisis politics), but remove the stale blunt “Napoleon + Hitler” equivalence. No racial/extermination ideology or Hitler-level evil. Giovanni remains morally culpable through organized crime, coercion, surveillance, trafficking/research abuses, political intimidation, and later aggression.
- The old Kanto movement tried to leave Indigo. Giovanni learns from its failure: do not abandon the institution; capture it. His politics are better described as Kanto restorationism/revanchism/hegemonism than simple separatism.
- He captures unified Indigo and gradually turns it into a Kanto-led order. Exact pace and whether this was always his plan versus an escalation under Johto resistance remain OPEN.

Years-long Viridian modernization before his challenge:
- Giovanni builds goodwill through genuine projects that begin in Viridian and may spread as a “Viridian model”: beginner-partner distribution, trainer/vocational schools, roads and underground routes, cheap Pokeballs that challenge Silph, public safety, trade relationships, Pokemon Center/emergency infrastructure, and related modernization.
- “Starters” means scalable first-partner programs, not automatically mass distribution of rare Bulbasaur/Charmander/Squirtle; exact species/program design is OPEN.
- Projects provide real benefits while also building registration, patronage, logistics, surveillance, institutional loyalty, and strategic capacity.
- Secret Rocket money supplies seed capital through crime proceeds, laundering, fronts, construction/logistics firms, hardliner donors, and corrupt procurement. Once growth begins, legitimate taxes/trade/manufacturing revenue can make projects self-sustaining. Do not imply Rocket indefinitely pays every public expense.
- League/government capture includes a mixed coalition: bribed officials, blackmailed officials, Kanto nationalists, careerists, honest supporters of successful programs, and unaware bureaucrats. Do not portray every official as knowingly corrupt.
- By the time Giovanni challenges Lance, much of Kanto believes he has already been doing the Champion's job.

Government terminology/structure to record:
- “Pokemon League” is the generic worldwide system.
- “Indigo League” is the joint Kanto-Johto top-level institution, including Champion/Elite Four authority, defense, Gym/trainer regulation, major interregional and international functions.
- “Kanto government” or “Kanto regional administration” handles Kanto civil policy such as roads, education, commerce, health, and local funding under the Indigo framework.
- Viridian city government is the municipal layer.
- Avoid “Indigo government” as the default term; use “Indigo League administration” or “League government/officials” contextually.
- The unified Champion must have checks and institutional mediation; a battle title cannot automatically grant unlimited power. Exact constitutional structure remains partly open.
